# 11 Frostvault (Space pack)

A cryogenics research vault of the invented Borealis Cryogenics: a hexagonal compound under a cold night sky with a
big ice-blue moon. Medium (a regular hexagon 128 m across, about 10,600 m2 inside), 16 players, every mode (FFA, TDM,
KotH, CTF, Gun Game). Generated by `tools/space/frostvault.py` (run from the repository root; a second argument draws a
plan PNG). Everything here is original: no film or game location, name or insignia.

## Layout

The whole map turns 180 degrees about the centre: every block, stair, prop, spawn and decal has a twin at (-x, -z),
turned 180 degrees. Blue holds the west intake, Red the east.

| Area | Where | What it is |

The podium can be mantled from the corridor and the pit from the podium. |

| Lab North / Lab South | (0, -+42), 26 x 12 m | White walk-in labs (hills 2 and 3 inside): benches down the middle, cold-storage lockers, a specimen tank, decon arches at the two south doors, a stair to the roof (parapet 0.5 m, steppable) and a railed bridge from the roof to the gallery. |
| Pod Hall A / Pod Hall B | (-28, -32) / (28, 32), 18 x 16 m | Roofless halls with two rows of four lying cryo pods, upright pods along the back wall, a coolant manifold and a console; doors on all four sides. |

Lanes from an intake: the middle (screen door, forecourt, corridor door, inner door, podium stairs, pit); the near pod
hall and the lab lane to the north lab, the server room and back to the far intake; the service alley behind the pod
hall to the north passage; and the upper route (corner stair to the gallery, gallery to a lab roof, lab stair down, or
the far corner stair down). The gallery loop overlooks the whole vault but is itself exposed to the lab roofs.

## Objectives

- Hills: the pit (0, 0, radius 6), Lab North (0, -42) and Lab South (0, 42), all on x = 0 (equal walks for both teams).
- Flags: (-52, 0) and (52, 0), in front of each intake's airlock.
- Team spawns: 8 per team, in the intakes.

## What to check in game

The pit and podium edges (mantling both ways), the corridor doors and the roofed loop's lighting, the gallery rails
(cover, not walls), the lab roof stairs and parapet step, the corner stairs' cheek walls, the signs over the doors, and
the night sky's moon against the white walls.
