# 09 Hollowrock Refinery (Space pack)

An ore refinery hollowed into an irregular asteroid, run by the invented Tessera Ore Combine, under a black starry sky
with a hard low sun and a dim gas giant. Medium-large (about 135 x 113 m, 11,750 m2 inside the outline), 16 players,
every mode. Generated by `tools/space/hollowrock.py` (run from the repository root; `python3 tools/space/hollowrock.py
. plan.png` also draws a plan). Everything is original: no film or game location, company, ship or insignia.

## Shape

A `smooth` boundary through 16 control points (point-symmetric) with 2.5 m noise, and a terrain `cliff` edge (14 m over
7 m): the rock's crags rise all round, so there is no wall. Real terrain (1 m cells, 185 x 163 samples) does the shaping:
a gentle dome, the raised docks, the yard terraces, the plateau terraces, spoil ridges over the drifts, mounds over the
chambers, and after the heights the tunnels (`cave`), the fall-kill chasms (`hole` with an absolute floor at -16 m), the
crucible and the ore pits. `"fallKill": -4`: the chasm floors are far below it, the drift floors (-3.5 m) above it.

Lanes for Blue (mirrored for Red): the main line (dock, ramp A, the bridge, the smelter), short and exposed to the
terrace above; the yard flank (dock, ramp B, the yard, down to the smelter's west, or round the chasm's south end to
the bridge landing); the terrace (dock, ramp C, the terrace, down its east ramp onto the hill 2 pad, which is the
enemy's tunnel exit side); the Neck (bridge landing north to the hill pad); and the drift, the covered flank that
comes out under the enemy's terrace. Both tunnel exits, both hill pads and the smelter make the middle a crossroads.

## Objectives

- Hills: the crucible (0, 0, radius 5), and (8.5, -16.5) and (-8.5, 16.5) (radius 4.5) on level pads either side of
  the smelter. The self-test measures the walks on the game's nav graph: hill 1 55/60 m, hills 2 and 3 62/57 and
  58/60 m, the enemy flag 107/110 m (Blue/Red).
- Flags: (-39.5, -31) and (39.5, 31), in front of the control huts.
- Team spawns: 12 per team: 8 on the dock, 2 in the yard, 1 at the terrace ramp, 1 at the dock's foot by the drift.

## Counts and cost

From the self-test: 818 boxes (154 drawn one by one: the huts, the bridges, the sign boards, the gantry posts; 8
hidden colliders inside the furnace stacks), 376 props (234 solid; 268 hand-placed plus Clutter), 41 meshes (stacks,
girders, railings), 314 decals placed of 336 asked for, 24 spawns, nav graph about 1,010 nodes (every link is walked by
the real mover because the map has a fall kill: build about 2.2 s on an idle 2-CPU container). No foliage. Destruction
off (`"destructible": false`).

## What to check in game

The glow in the crucible and the furnace throats, the bridge's railing against the rift, the drift's light fading
away from its mouths, the skylight drop in the yard, the view from the terrace, and that the cliff edge reads as the
rock's crags against the starfield.
