# 03 Keelhaul Yard (Space pack)

An orbital breaker's yard of the invented Verrin Salvage company, at night under a gas giant. The bow section of a
derelict hauler (the Keel) is moored to the yard's deck, and a captured asteroid (the Rock) hangs over the middle on two
mooring masts. The yard's gravity plating is failing in patches, so one map holds four kinds of gravity: this is the
showcase for [gravity zones](../README.md#gravity-zones). Medium (about 107 x 105 m inside the outline, 8,200 m2),
16 players, every mode. Generated by `tools/space/keelhaul.py` (run from the repository root; `python3 tools/space/keelhaul.py . plan.png`
also draws a plan). Everything is original: no film or game location, ship, company or insignia.

## Layout

Mirror-symmetric about z = 0: Blue holds the north dock (z < 0), Red the south. Everything hangs over a void
(`"fallKill": -4`; the yard deck is at y = 0), inside a smooth boundary with no edge.

| Area | Where | Gravity | What's there |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Yard | the middle deck, x -16..30, z +-30 | plain | Hill 1 under the Rock; cut plate stacks, cutter rigs, cargo pods, barriers and plating nodes as cover; the foot of both masts |
| The Hull | the hauler's hull plate, x = 30, 72 x 26 m | sideways: down is +X | The whole wall is a floor you fight on. Steel ribs and a brass ledge are cover in its frame; hill 3 is on it at 16 m up; a lip at the top and the ends keeps you on |
| The Bowl | a quarter pipe from the yard deck up onto the hull, 24 m wide | turns in three 30-degree steps | Nine solid slabs under three zones; walk (or run) from the deck straight up onto the wall. Bots use it |
| The Hold | the cargo hold, sunk 3 m, x -46..-16, z +-24 | a third of normal (7 m/s2) | An 18 m void hatch across the middle with a railed catwalk over it (hill 2); container stacks you can jump onto only here; ramps up to the yard at both ends |
| The Rock | a 12 m asteroid centred 9 m over the yard's middle | its own pull (18 m/s2 towards its centre, 8 m out) | Walk round and under it; a crater on its underside, scattered rocks, two brass cleats. Reached by the piers |
| The Mooring Masts | columns at z = +-14.5 with switchback stairs (15 m) and a top walkway | plain | A pier runs from each mast's top to just short of the Rock's top; a 0.2 m step off the pier's end onto the Rock |
| North / South Dock | x +-16, z +-30..46 | plain | The dock office (a U of walls with two doors and a roof) holding the flag and four spawns; four more spawns outside; tanks, pods, barriers, beacons |

Getting between gravities:
- **Deck to hull:** walk up the Bowl, or jump anywhere within 10 m of the hull (its zone starts half a metre up): the
  plating grabs you and you land on the wall. **Hull to deck:** walk down past the wall's bottom edge (a half-metre drop
  onto the deck), or down the Bowl.
- **Deck to Rock:** the mast stairs and the pier (bots), or get thrown up into its pull. **Rock to deck:** jump off the
  side away from the pier (you fall once you're 8 m from its centre; the yard is 3 m below its bottom) or walk back
  onto a pier.
- **Yard to hold:** jump down 3 m anywhere along the hold's east edge (a lip marks it), or the ramps at the ends.
  **Hold to yard:** the ramps (a jump here reaches 2.4 m, not 3).

Lanes: the docks join the yard through two 4 m doors each; the yard's middle lane runs dock to dock under the Rock
(hill 1); the hull is a tall flank with sight over the whole yard (and a hill of its own); the hold is the low flank
with the catwalk choke and long low-gravity jumps round it; the masts and piers put a third level over the middle.

## Objectives

- Hills: (0, 0, 0) under the Rock, (-31, -3, 0) on the hold's catwalk, (29.95, 16, 0) on the hull wall; all on z = 0.
- Flags: (0, 0, -42) and (0, 0, 42), inside the dock offices.
- Team spawns: 8 per team (4 in the office, 4 outside).
- Walks (nav graph): hill 1 43/45 m, hill 2 59/60 m, hill 3 64/64 m, enemy flag 87/87 m from Blue/Red.

## Gravity zones (the file's `gravity` field)

In order (the first zone containing a position wins): the hull above the Bowl, the hull's two sides (from 0.5 m up),
the Bowl's three steps (30, 60, 90 degrees), the two piers (plain, only the deck's footprint, listed before the Rock so they stay level), the
Rock's sphere (point field, radius 8), the hold (uniform, 7 m/s2). The default is plain gravity.

## How it's built (cost)

- 283 boxes (drawn), 57 meshes (46 solid: the Rock, its rocks and the Bowl's nine slabs), 102 props, about 380 decals,
  16 spawns, 95 waypoints; nav about 900 nodes, built in about a second. No buildings, no terrain, no movers.
- Decals: the pack's Gravity set (`Maps/Space/Decals/Gravity`, by `tools/space/gravity_decals.py`): the Verrin Salvage
  mark, plating-fault / low-gravity / hull-plating-live signs, plating seams, arrows; plus global stains, hazard
  stripes, vents, panels and the Stratos pad ring.
- Props: `Props/Space/Gravity` (by `tools/space/gravity_props.py`: plating node, cutter rig, mooring clamp, cut plate stack,
  warning beacon, scrap; shown on the Dev map **Gravity Props**), `Props/Station`, `Props/Industrial`.
- Sky: a night preset with a big moon, stars and no clouds (no horizon: the void below is the sky's ground colour).

## What to check in game

Walk up the Bowl and look back at the yard from the hull (the horizon rolls over about a quarter of a second at each
of the three edges); throw a grenade on the hull (it arcs along the wall); sprint-jump the hold's hatch; walk a pier
onto the Rock and all the way round to its underside; watch bots take the Bowl and the piers.
