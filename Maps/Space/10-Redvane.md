# 10 Redvane Processor (Space pack)

A terraforming atmosphere processor of the invented Meridian Terraforming Works on a red dust plain, thickening a thin
atmosphere under a hazy ochre sky (the `desert` preset with a strong dust haze, low dusty clouds, the sun at 16:12).
Medium (136 x 110 m, about 11,500 m2 inside the outline), 16 players, every mode. Generated by `tools/space/redvane.py`
(run from the repository root; `python3 tools/space/redvane.py . plan.png` also draws a plan). Everything is original:
no film or game location, company or insignia.

## Shape

An `ellipse` boundary (radii 68 x 54 m) with a little noise (1.2 m) and a steel `fence` edge (1.8 m, posts every 3 m);
shots fly out over it (barrier height 2.5 m). Flat ground (a `Ground` op), so everything stands on boxes and meshes.

Lanes for Blue (mirrored for Red): the main lane between the fans to the plenum under the stack and the Gallery ramp; north round the
fan to the vehicle yard and on to the near cooling tower (hill 2) and its pools; south round the other fan through the
pipe field to the far tower (hill 3) and the enemy's pools. Both towers open on all four sides and the plenum is open
between its legs all round, with the Gallery above it reached from both teams' sides, so every objective can be attacked two ways; the Crown looks down on everything but has
only the two narrow ramps.

## Objectives

- Hills: the plenum under the stack (0, 0, radius 8) and the two cooling towers (0, -34) and (0, 34) (radius 4.5).
  The self-test measures the walks on the game's nav graph to nodes at each hill's own height (see the summary line
  it prints for the current numbers; all within 12% Blue/Red).
- Flags: (-44.5, 0) and (44.5, 0), at the bunkers' doors.
- Team spawns: 10 per team round the bunker.

## Counts and cost

From the self-test: 776 boxes (300 drawn one by one: the bunkers, the hangars and containers, the tower plinths, the
gantries, the ramps, the pool rims and pipe supports; 47 hidden colliders under the pipes, inside the stack, the towers
and the railings), 308 props (194 solid; 200 hand-placed plus Clutter), 523 meshes (pipes, railings, the stack and
tower drums), 462 decals placed of 471 asked for, 20 spawns, nav graph about 1,220 nodes (build about 90 ms). No
foliage; destruction off.

## Assets

- General: `Props/Space/Hollowrock` (the Space set, written by `tools/space/hollowrock_redvane_props.py`; here the
  Intake Fan, Condenser Unit, Pipe Valve Stack, Reagent Sphere, Atmos Sensor Pole, Warning Light Mast and Hab
  Container), plus the Facility, Industrial, Mining, Military, Station, Utility, Volatile and Debris sets.
- Pack-local decals: `Maps/Space/Decals/Redvane` (the intake rings, the stack ring, pipe lanes, dust rings, tide marks,
  vapour warnings, the Meridian logo and the tiled place-name signs; `tools/space/hollowrock_redvane_decals.py`).

## What to check in game

The fans turning behind their grilles, the haze against the stack's crown, the Gallery's railing from below, the pools'
water against their rims, the pipe field's gaps (mantle over the low runs, not the racks), and that the fence reads as
the plant's edge against the dust horizon.
