# 04 Twin Shard (Space pack)

A binary asteroid: two 36 m shards of one broken rock, each with its own pull, mined by the invented Oresong Collective.
Blue's camp is on the west shard, Red's on the east. Two tethers join them: a low-gravity catwalk over the top and an
upside-down one underneath, with a little moonlet hanging between. A second showcase for
[gravity zones](../README.md#gravity-zones), built around two point fields (not one planet, as on Pocket Planet).
Medium (about 124 x 65 m inside the outline, 7,200 m2), 16 players, every mode. Generated by `tools/space/twinshard.py`
(run from the repository root; `python3 tools/space/twinshard.py . plan.png` also draws a plan). Everything is original.

## Layout

Mirror-symmetric about x = 0. The shards' centres are at (+-34, 24, 0), 18 m in radius; their pull reaches 26 m out.
Between and beyond the wells gravity is plain, straight down into the void (`"fallKill": -4`, 28 m under the centres).

| Area | Where | Gravity | What's there |
|---|---|---|---|
| West / East Shard | (+-34, 24, 0) | 18 m/s2 towards its centre | The team's world: walk all the way round it. A camp of three habitat pods, a mast and cleats round the top pole (eight spawns, the flag on the Line's landing over the pole); a drill mast with ore crates on the outer flank; 44 rocks; a brass ring round the bottom pole where the Keel Line lands |
| The Line | y = 42.2, pole to pole | low (6 m/s2) | A railed 3 m catwalk joining the top poles. A sprint jump flies about 25 m. Hill 1 is on the Knot in the middle |
| The Knot | (0, 42.2, 0), a 12 m pad | low | A pad round a 4 m open eye (the Drop); a railed walkway off its south side to a platform (hill 3) |
| The Keel Line | y = 5.8, pole to pole | upside down: down is +Y | Its underside is the floor. Walk off a shard's bottom pole and along it hanging under the deck, rails "above" you; hill 2 is on its middle pad. Its top face is an ordinary floor you can fall onto |
| The Pebble | (0, 24, 0), 8 m across | 14 m/s2 towards it, reaching 0.8 m over its surface | Drop through the Knot's eye and land on it; jump off its underside and you fall onto the Keel Line's top (players only: bots don't drop) |

The loop: from your camp walk down the shard's side to its bottom pole, along the Keel Line upside down, up the other
shard to its top and back over the Line. Short cuts: the Drop through the Knot's eye to the Pebble and on down to the
Keel Line's top; a sprint jump along the Line; falling off the Line's middle (outside the Knot's eye) is fatal.

## Objectives

- Hills (on x = 0): the Knot (0, 42.2, -4), the Keel Line's middle (0, 5.8, 0, upside down), the Knot's side platform
  (0, 42.2, 17).
- Flags: on the Line's landings over the top poles, (+-34, 42.2, 0).
- Team spawns: 8 per shard, on its upper half round the camp.
- Walks (nav graph): hill 1 57/60 m, hill 2 83/82 m, hill 3 71/70 m, enemy flag 93/92 m from Blue/Red.

## Gravity zones (the file's `gravity` field)

In order: the Line (a box only as wide as its deck, so the shards' tops either side keep their pull), the Knot and its
platform, the Keel Line (a box under its deck, gravity +Y), the Pebble (a sphere 4.8 m out), the west shard, the east
shard (spheres 26 m out). The Line's and the Keel Line's boxes stop 3 m short of the poles, so each tether joins its
shard inside the shard's own pull and the walk on and off is a gentle curve. The default is plain gravity.

## How it's built (cost)

- 57 boxes (tethers, pads, rails), 137 meshes (131 solid: the shards, the Pebble, rocks, pods, masts, cleats, crates),
  20 props (near the top poles only: props are drawn upright), about 110 decals, 16 spawns, 69 waypoints; nav about
  1,950 nodes, built in about half a second. No buildings, no terrain, no movers.
- Shards are solid icosphere meshes, and everything on them is a solid mesh stood along the surface by its own yaw and
  pitch: a map has one `Planet` op, so the Surface ops can't dress two rocks.
- Decals and props: the pack's Gravity set and `Props/Space` (see 03 Keelhaul Yard), plus `Props/Station`.
- Sky: a night preset with stars, a dim moon and no clouds.

## What to check in game

Walk from your camp round the shard and onto the Keel Line (the horizon rolls the whole way round); drop through the
Knot's eye onto the Pebble; jump off the Pebble's underside onto the Keel Line; sprint-jump along the Line; watch bots
walk the Keel Line upside down.
