# 07 Canopy Deck (Space pack)

The arboretum deck of the invented generation ship Long Meridian, derelict for centuries: a biodome inside a torn hull,
open to the stars where the dome has gone, the ship's ribs still arching over the deck and the emergency lighting still
blinking on them. The soil has gone wild: grass, shrubs and trees have taken the terraces, the dry irrigation canals and
the airlock aprons, while the hydroponic racks on the upper terraces still glow. Medium-large (about 126 x 97 m inside
an ellipse, 9,400 m2), 16 players, every mode. Generated by `tools/space/arboretum.py` (run from the repository root;
`python3 tools/space/arboretum.py . plan.png` also draws a plan). Everything is original: no film or game location,
ship, name or insignia.

## Layout

Point-symmetric about the centre: everything Blue has at (x, z) Red has at (-x, -z), turned 180 degrees. Blue holds the
west airlock bay, Red the east one.

| Area | Where | Height | What's there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Airlock bays (West / East) | (-+54, 0) | 0.6 apron | A hangar-like bay with wide doors facing the deck and side doors, the CTF flag inside, cargo pods, cases, a console, the seed vault; the team's spawns inside and behind it, more on the apron |
| The orchard | x -+30..-46 | 0 | Trees, planter troughs, soil heaps, sprinkler masts, garden junk and a wrecked drone: the way from the bay to the canal, with cover |
| Dry canals | x = -+25..28, the whole depth | -1.6 | Sunken lanes curving north-south, with sluice gates across them at z = -+14, algae and puddles in the bed, vines on the banks, an irrigation pump station on the inner bank |
| Central terrace | r 14 round (0, 0) | 1.4 | The great tree in the middle (hill 1 round its trunk), planter troughs, a lit rack, cargo and lamps; four ramps up on the diagonals and banks you can mantle |
| North / South terrace | (0, -+37), 44 x 12 | 2.4 | The hydroponic bays: two rows of racks either side of hill 2 / hill 3, seed vault cabinets, consoles, fan units and a bulkhead panel with the deck signs; ramps at both ends and in the middle |
| Collapsed walkways | (-+9, -+17..-+31) | 4.2 | A railed bridge from each upper terrace towards the centre whose far end has fallen: a slab of rubble steps (a Ramp op under a tilted slab) leads down to the deck. High ground over the centre |
| Hull ribs | 3.5 m inside the outline | 0-13 | Pillars with cosmetic beams arching inward, pipe runs and cable trays along the hull between them: the perimeter lane |

Lanes from Blue to Red: through the centre terrace (short, exposed, the hill fight); along either upper terrace (long,
racks for cover, the walkways for height); the canals as sunken cross-links between the terraces and the centre; and
the perimeter lane along the ribs. Blue's spawns look east through the bay door at the terrace bank, which blocks the
low lines; the upper terraces don't see into the bays.

## Objectives

- Hills: the centre (0, 3.5) radius 6, beside the great tree's trunk (on the mirror line x = 0, clear of the trunk); the north and south terraces (0, -+37) radius 5. All on the
  mirror line x = 0, so the walks are equal (the self-test measures Blue/Red 56/52, 68/66, 68/68 m).
- Flags: inside the bays, (-+55, 0).
- Team spawns: 11 per team (inside and behind the bay, on the apron and in the orchard), plus 6 free-for-all spawns on
  the terraces and by the centre. 28 spawns in all.

## How it's built (cost)

- Terrain (1 m cells, 22 features): light noise, the two canals (valleys), the central plateau and four ramps, the
  two upper terraces with three ramps each, the aprons under the bays, pads under the fallen walkways.
- About 120 boxes drawn one by one (the bays, ribs, bridges, stairs, rubble steps, bulkhead panels, the trunk) plus
  tree trunks and prop colliders (about 420 solid boxes in all); 114 meshes (rib beams, the canopy, deck plates, the
  fallen slabs); 236 placed props (172 solid); about 380 decals; about 19,000 plants (28 trees).
- Sky: the `night` preset with a big ochre moon (a gas giant), strong stars and raised ambient; the hull is the
  ellipse's wall edge (10 m, metal) and the view above is open.
- Destruction off: the ship is metal.

## What to check in game

The bay doors and signs, the canal beds (sand under the algae), the ramps' slopes, the walkway stairs and rubble steps,
the rib beams meeting the hull wall, and that the great tree's canopy sits over the trunk.
