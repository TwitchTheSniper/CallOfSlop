# 12 Vesper Strip (Space pack)

A night-time spaceport bazaar of the invented Port Vesper authority: a bean-shaped strip under a starry sky with a big
rust-coloured gas giant and a pink haze on the horizon, lit by neon. Medium (a smooth bean 148 x 100 m, about
10,000 m2 inside), 16 players, every mode (FFA, TDM, KotH, CTF, Gun Game). Generated by `tools/space/vesperstrip.py`
(run from the repository root; a second argument draws a plan PNG). Everything here is original: no film or game
location, ship, name or insignia (the shops are Kestrel Cantina, Zero-G Noodles, Torque & Sprocket, Nova Arcade, Capsule
Inn, Duty Free, Pawn, Fuel; all invented).

## Layout

Mirror-symmetric about x = 0: every block, stair, solid prop and spawn has a twin at (-x, z) with its yaw negated, so
both teams get the same cover; only the neon signs and the litter differ. Blue holds the west lobe, Red the east.

Lanes from a base: the main gate to the plaza (straight, broken by the transit kiosk); the cargo gate along the
concourse and the pads (long and open, with the shuttles as cover); through the repair shop into the market lane and
along the shop fronts to the customs hall; and the rooftop lane, which overlooks the market lane and the plaza but is
open to fire from the pads side.

## Objectives

- Hills: the plaza (0, -6, radius 5), the centre pad (0, -36, radius 5) and the customs hall (0, 18, radius 4.5).
- Flags: (-67, 0) and (67, 0), at the back of each base.
- Team spawns: 8 per team.

## Counts (from the generator and the self-test)

About 260 map boxes (about 280 drawn), 48 cosmetic meshes (the dark towers and spires beyond the edge), about 310
hand-placed props plus mirrored and unmirrored clutter (the pack-local Vesper set, 19 props, plus Retail, Street,
Urban, Station, Animated, Industrial and Facility props), about 360 decals (the pack-local Vesper set: neon signs, pad
rings and numbers, market tiles, the plaza mosaic, scrawls, posters, the Port Vesper emblem; plus global stains,
posters and road marks). Destruction off. Nav graph about 1,000 nodes.

## What to check in game

The neon signs over the shop fronts (tiles lined up, glow), the rooftop lane's bridges and parapet steps, the stairs
beside the repair shops, the shuttles' collision (hull, wings, skids), the customs roof's thick edge with its signs,
the gas giant and pink haze against the dark towers outside the wall, and the fuel bowsers near the centre pad.
