# 08 Ignition Hall (Space pack)

The containment hall of the invented Halvard Fusion Works' tokamak: an octagonal hall round the core, with a turbine
wing on each side. Orange and blue everywhere: hazard stripes, warning decals, the field coils' blue lines, the plasma
column glowing up the middle. Medium-large (about 148 x 98 m, 9,250 m2 inside a 64-segment polygon), 16 players,
every mode. Generated by `tools/space/reactor.py` (run from the repository root; `python3 tools/space/reactor.py .
plan.png` also draws a plan). Everything is original: no film or game location, company, name or insignia.

## Layout

Point-symmetric about the centre: everything Blue has at (x, z) Red has at (-x, -z). Blue holds the west turbine wing
(Turbine Hall A), Red the east one (Turbine Hall B). Concentric rings out from the middle:

| Ring | Where | Height | What's there |
|---|---|---|---|
| The core plinth | r 6 | 0.5 | A step up (walk on from any side) round the plasma column (a solid pillar 10 m tall with copper rings): hill 1 |
| The containment torus | r 7-10 | 0.5-3.5 | Solid all the way round except four 3 m injector gaps on the diagonals: the only ways onto the plinth, and it blocks every straight line through the middle |
| Field coils and the coolant mains | r 11-37 on the axes | 1.2-3.2 | A coil block on each axis against the torus, then a chain of glowing conduit (waist-high: mantle it) running out along each axis with a gap and a valve manifold half way: the hall's four quadrants |
| The quadrants | between the mains | 0 | Pump bays, cryo dewars, propellant tanks, gas cages, pods and carts: cover on the way round |
| North / South control galleries | (0, -+34..-+47), 32 m wide | 0 / 3.5 / 6.7 | A control room on the floor under a deck on pillars (hill 2 / hill 3 behind its parapet, consoles looking over the hall) with stairs up at both ends, and a smaller observation deck above it with its own stair |
| Catwalks and the core walkway | x = 0, z -+5..-+40; r 1-5 at the centre | 6.6 | From each observation deck a railed catwalk runs out over the torus to a square walkway round the top of the column: the high route between the two galleries, looking down on hill 1 |
| Turbine wings | x -+49..-+74, z -+13 | 0 | A wall across the mouth with a blast door portal (leaf slid open), two turbine generators, pumps, dewars, tanks, a crane beam; the flag at the far end and the team's spawns round it |
| The hull | the octagon | 0-12 | Corner pillars with lamps and signs, blast shutters and pipe runs on the diagonal walls, a dark ceiling with radial beams over everything |

Lanes from Blue to Red: through the hall along the west main to the torus, round to a diagonal gap and over the plinth
(hill 1); up either gallery's near stair to hill 2 or 3 and on down the far stair; or up to the observation deck and
across the catwalks over the core. The torus blocks the line from wing mouth to wing mouth; the coil blocks and the
mains break up the quadrants.

## Objectives

- Hills: the plinth (0, 0, 0.5) radius 6; the lower galleries (0, -+39, 3.5) radius 4.5. All on x = 0, so the walks
  are equal (the self-test measures Blue/Red 74/67, 93/92, 88/94 m).
- Flags: at the far end of each wing, (-+70, 0).
- Team spawns: 10 per team (in the wing and the two hall corners beside it), plus 4 free-for-all spawns in front of
  the galleries. 24 spawns in all.

## How it's built (cost)

- About 130 boxes drawn one by one (gallery pillars, decks, parapets, rails, stairs, back walls, the wing mouths,
  corner pillars, catwalks) plus 29 hidden colliders (the plinth strips, the column, 20 torus segments) and prop
  colliders (about 480 solid boxes in all); 93 meshes (the plinth, column and rings, the torus, the ceiling and its
  beams, the crane beams); 260 placed props (186 solid); about 320 decals.
- Sky: the `night` preset, dim stars, a warm high sun and raised ambient; the ceiling disc is a cosmetic mesh.
- Destruction off: the plant is steel.

## What to check in game

The torus against its colliders at the injector gaps, the catwalk joining the observation deck and the walkway round
the column, the gallery stairs, the blast door portals in the wing mouths, the signs on the back walls and the ceiling
beams against the hull wall.
