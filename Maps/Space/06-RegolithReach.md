# 06 Regolith Reach (Space pack)

A regolith mining outpost of the invented Pallas Ore Concern on the floor of a crater, under a black starry sky with a
low harsh sun. Medium (about 150 x 121 m, 15,100 m2 inside the outline), 16 players, Team Deathmatch / KotH / CTF /
FFA / Gun Game. Generated by `tools/space/regolith_reach.py` (run from the repository root; `python3
tools/space/regolith_reach.py . plan.png` also draws a plan). Everything is original: no film or game location, ship,
company or insignia.

## Layout

Point-symmetric: the west half (terrain features, buildings, props, decals, spawns) is turned 180 degrees about the
centre for the east half, and the boulder clutter is mirrored, so both teams get the same ground, cover and walks.
Blue holds **Hab West**, Red **Hab East**. Grey-white regolith (`grass` painted pale, slopes darker, rock on the
steepest), hazard orange and yellow on the machines, white hab modules.

| Area | Where | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| The excavation | the middle, a 40 m bowl 3.5 m deep with a level floor | Hill 1, two orange core drill rigs, a conveyor, mine carts, boulders, a tracked excavator on the west ramp. In from the west and east by 7 m vehicle ramps, from the north and south by the conveyor ramps. |
| Ore docks | (0, -+33), pads 0.4 m up | Hills 2 and 3: regolith hoppers, an ore bin, a floodlight mast, blasting crates, the ore conveyor climbing down into the pit. |
| Hab West / East | (-+52, 0), a 1.2 m pad | A Station Control Hub (two storeys) and a Station Hangar Bay garage, the flag between them, two sealed domes, a glazed pressurised tunnel from the hub pad out to an airlock module at x = -+29 (a covered lane with a window band to shoot through and mantle into), rovers, oxygen tanks, an antenna mast and a sensor dish. 12 spawns a side. |
| Survey hab | (-+20, -+24) | A Station Crew Quarters with roof access (the only roof you can reach), airlock, cabinets. |
| Solar farm | (-+26, -+43) | 20 tilted solar array racks in four staggered rows: chest-high cover in a grid. |
| Landing pad | (-+32, +-30) | A parked lunar lander on a ringed pad, floor lights, beacons, a hover sled, a cargo pod. |
| Between | | Berms 2-2.4 m high across the quadrants, three small craters with rims, boulder fields, survey lights, scattered crates. |

Lanes west to east: north past the survey hab and the solar farm to the north ore dock; the middle through the pit;
south past the landing pad and the crater bowl to the south ore dock; with the berms, craters and the tunnel as the
cover and the cross-links between them. The crater wall round the whole site is the boundary's `cliff` edge (10 m
over 8 m).

## Objectives

- Hills: the pit floor (0, 0), radius 5.5, and the ore docks (0, -31) and (0, 31), radius 4.5 (on the ground).
- Flags: (-58, 2) and (58, -2), between each hab's hub and garage.
- Team spawns: 12 per team on the hab pads.

## How it's built (cost)

- Terrain: 1 m cells, 205 x 175 samples, 41 features (noise, the basin, berm ridges, crater hills and basins, pads,
  ramps), the dirt material painting the ramps and rover tracks. 10 boxes drawn one by one (the two tunnels), 6
  buildings, about 940 props (350 solid; 550 of them mirrored boulder and rubble clutter), about 90 meshes (domes,
  tunnel tubes and mullions, sign boards), about 370 decals (signs, logos, landing rings, ore arrows, survey marks,
  scuffs, dust, tyre tracks and footprints).
- Nav graph: about 1,300 nodes (AutoWaypoints every 7 m plus hand-placed ones in the pockets), about 1 s to build
  on the shared box.
- Destruction off.

## What to check in game

The sky (black with stars, no horizon glow), the pale regolith against the hazard colours, the pit ramps' slopes, the
tunnels' windows, the domes' seams with the ground, the solar rows as cover, and that the crater wall reads as a wall.
