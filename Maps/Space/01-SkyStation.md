# 01 Stratos Station (Space pack)

A cloud-mining station of the invented Halcyon Gas Combine, floating in a gas giant's upper atmosphere at dusk. Large
(about 136 x 252 m inside the outline), 20 players, Team Deathmatch / KotH / CTF / FFA. Generated by
`tools/space/skystation.py` (run from the repository root; `python3 tools/space/skystation.py . plan.png` also draws a
plan). Inspired by "floating sky city" pictures the user gave (round platforms on stalks, domes, railed and covered
bridges, landing pads, a cloud sea at sunset) and by a node-graph layout sketch; everything here is original: no film or
game location, ship, name or insignia.

## Layout

Mirror-symmetric about z = 0: Blue (North Dock) holds the north, Red (South Dock) the south. Sixteen platforms:

| Platform | Centre | Radius | Deck | What's on it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North / South Dock | (0, -+96) | 22 | 30.0 | Station Control Hub (two storeys, roof with a domed tower and a halo ring), spawns, the CTF flag in front |
| Refinery | (0, -+50) | 14 | 28.0 | Refinery Pump House, two big gas tanks, pipe runs, a volatile canister rack |
| Quarters | (-40, -+78) | 12 | 29.0 | Station Crew Quarters (two storeys, roof access), two spawns |
| Tank Farm | (-52, -+40) | 10 | 27.5 | three gas tanks, pipes, a canister rack |
| Hangar | (42, -+82) | 15 | 29.5 | Station Hangar Bay (tall bay, 5 m arches, a mezzanine), a hover sled, two spawns |
| Comms | (52, -+42) | 9 | 27.5 | antenna mast, sensor dish, a small domed tower |
| The Hub | (0, 0) | 20 (ring, open eye 11.5) | 26.0 | four tanks, barriers, lamps; four spokes to the core |
| Hub Core | (0, 0) | 7 | 26.0 | hill 1, a beacon mast |
| West / East Pad | (-+50, 0) | 12 | 25.0 | landing pads with markings and a parked cloud skimmer; hills 2 and 3 |

Bridges (per half): Dock - Refinery (open, 4 m), Refinery - Hub (covered walkway with windows, 4 m), Dock - Quarters and
Dock - Hangar (open, 3.2 m), Quarters - Tank Farm (catwalk, 2 m), Tank Farm - West Pad (open, 3 m), Tank Farm -
Refinery (a long exposed catwalk, 1.8 m), Hangar - Comms (open, 3 m), Comms - East Pad (catwalk, 2 m); in the middle Hub
- West Pad (catwalk, 2.2 m) and Hub - East Pad (open, 3 m).

Lanes: the main line (Dock - Refinery - covered walkway - Hub) is short and covered; the west flank (Quarters, Tank
Farm, West Pad) is longer with the Tank Farm catwalk as a cross-link back to the Refinery; the east flank (Hangar,
Comms, East Pad) is wide open bridges. The hub ring links both pads, so the pads are the fights on the flanks.

Elevation: the decks step down from the docks (30 m) to the pads (25 m), so every bridge climbs or falls (gently on the
diagonals, where the collision steps under a tilted deck).

## Falling

`"fallKill": 15`: below 15 m you die. There's no ground op, so under the decks is open air down to the cloud puffs.
Railings (a collider band 0.45 - 1.1 m above the deck: bullets and grenades go under it) run round every rim and along
every bridge, cut only where bridges join; the hub's eye is railed too. So you fall only by jumping over a railing,
mantling out of a walkway window or being thrown by a blast (the volatile canister racks are next to the drops).
Bots: no nav link crosses the void, and nodes need floor under their whole footprint (see Maps/README.md, Void maps).

## Objectives

- Hills: Hub Core (0, 0), West Pad (-47.5, 0), East Pad (47.5, 0), all on z = 0 (equal walks), radius 4.5.
- Flags: (0, -86) and (0, 86), on the dock decks in front of the control hubs.
- Team spawns: 12 per team (8 round the dock, 2 on Quarters, 2 on Hangar), plus the control hub's spawn anchors.

## How it's built (cost)

- Decks: cosmetic cylinder / tube meshes (top 2 cm above) over hidden `Collider` strips (0.4 m strips at the round ends,
  1.5 m through the middle). Saucer undersides (inverted cones), bronze rim bands, collars, window lights and stalks
  down into the clouds are meshes. Railings are instanced box and cylinder meshes.
- About 4,400 hidden colliders (physics only, no draw calls), 0 drawn map boxes, about 3,800 meshes (instanced),
  8 buildings, about 130 placed props plus furniture, about 100 decals plus deck-panel scatters.
- Sky: `Skies/stratos_dusk.png`, a 2048 x 1024 panorama painted by `tools/space/make_stratos_sky.py` (violet zenith,
  storm clouds lit by a low western sun, a cloud sea below the horizon); its sun (elevation 4.5, azimuth 262) lights the
  map. A ring of 110 big flattened cloud spheres under the station gives the drop some depth.
- Destruction off (`"destructible": false`): the station is metal.

## What to check in game

The smoke views `space_station` (the whole station over the clouds), `space_station_walkway` (the covered walkway to the
hub) and `space_station_dock` (the North Dock's control hub). Look at the deck edges (mesh vs. collision), the railings,
the bridge joints, the sky's horizon line against the cloud puffs, and the fps over the cloud sea.
