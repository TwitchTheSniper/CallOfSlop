# 05 Keelspar Drydock (Space pack)

An orbital shipyard of the invented Meridian Hullworks: hull MH-07, half built, lies in its cradle along the middle of
the dock with nothing but the void under it. Medium (about 154 x 77 m, 10,400 m2 inside the outline), 16 players, Team
Deathmatch / KotH / CTF / FFA / Gun Game. Generated by `tools/space/keelspar_drydock.py` (run from the repository
root; `python3 tools/space/keelspar_drydock.py . plan.png` also draws a plan). Everything is original: no film or game
ship, yard, name or insignia.

## Layout

Point-symmetric: the whole map is one half (the north quay, the bow dock, the west gantry, the north scaffold, the west
hatch stair) turned 180 degrees about the centre, so both teams get the same. Blue holds the **Bow Dock** (west, x -70
to -50), Red the **Stern Dock** (east). Four deck heights:

| Deck | Height | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| Keel deck | 18 m | The floor inside the hull, from the bow ribs (x -40) through the plated midsection (x -24..24, bulkheads with offset doors at x -+20) out through the stern ribs to x 40. Open to the sides where the ribs are (a knee-high kick plate, then the drop). |
| Quays and docks | 20 m | North and South Quay (x -50..50, 19 m deep): the Dock Office (Station Crew Quarters, roof access), the Parts Store (pump house), weld bays by the hull edge, plate racks and bin racks along the back, gantry legs and stair towers. The docks: a Station Control Hub and a Fab Shop hangar each, the flag between them. |
| Scaffold decks | 24 m | Grid decks hung along both sides of the plated hull, a stair at each end up from the quay, and a stair at x = -+10 up the hull's flank onto the spine. |
| Spine and gantries | 28 m | The 3 m catwalk along the top of the hull (x -40..40) with the crown deck in the middle (hill 1), crossed by the two gantry crane bridges at x = -+20 that run quay to quay from a stair tower on each; a ramp from each end of the spine down to the dock. |

Links between the decks (per half, the other half turned): rib gangways quay -> keel deck at x = -32 and -38 (and the
same on the east), cradle arms quay -> hull side doors at x = -+12, the keel gangway from the ribs' end to the dock
(18 -> 20) under the spine ramp (28 -> 20), the two hatch stairs inside the hull up to the crown deck, the scaffold
stairs, and the gantry towers (two switchback flights each).

Lanes bow to stern: the two quays (long, dressed with cover, hills 2 and 3 in their middles), the keel deck through
the ribs and the hull (low, dark, the shortest), the spine (high and exposed, the crown deck on it), with the gantries
and scaffolds as cross-links at every quarter.

## Falling

`"fallKill": 6`: below 6 m you die. There's no ground op: between the quays and the hull (a 3.7 m gap), under the
gangways and beyond every deck edge is open void down to the cosmetic dock structure far below. Railings (a collider
band 0.45 - 1.1 m above the deck: bullets and grenades go under it) run round every quay, dock, scaffold, landing,
bridge and ramp, cut only where something joins; the keel deck's rib sections have only a kick plate, so a blast there
throws you off. Bots: no nav link crosses the void (the self-test checks every link).

## Objectives

- Hills: the crown deck (0, 0, 28 m), the North Quay (0, -20, 20 m) and the South Quay (0, 20, 20 m), radius 4.5.
- Flags: (-64, 20, 0) and (64, 20, 0), on the docks between the hub and the hangar.
- Team spawns: 12 per team round the dock's hub and hangar.

## How it's built (cost)

- 31 boxes drawn one by one (the decks, bulkheads, landings), about 2,100 hidden colliders (gangway slices, stairs,
  railings, the hull's inner walls and ceiling, mesh cores), about 1,500 instanced meshes (the hull tube and ribs, the
  bow cone and engine bells, railing panels, step boxes, gantry beams, the dock structure below), 8 buildings, about
  200 placed props (plus furniture), about 100 decals plus plate-seam and scorch scatters.
- Sky: the `night` preset with a black starry zenith, a 28-degree planet near the horizon and a harsh high sun.
- Nav graph: about 1,230 nodes, built in about 0.3 s on the user's PC.
- Destruction off (`"destructible": false`): the yard is metal.

## What to check in game

The hull's inside (the tube's inner face), the hatch housings on top of the hull, the gantry towers' two flights, the
spine ramps over the keel gangways, railing joints at every gangway mouth, and the fps looking down the dock from a
gantry.
