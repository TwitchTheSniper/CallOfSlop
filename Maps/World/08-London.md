# 08 London (medium, UK, 16 players)

**Theme (lead's choice for "a UK map in medium size"):** an East London neighbourhood on a grey afternoon: a canal with
a towpath, narrowboats and a lock; a Victorian railway viaduct whose arches are let as a bar, a garage and workshops,
with a small station in the middle; a high street with a corner pub, a barber and a corner shop; bay-fronted
terraces; a raised square with railings; a council tower block over the canal. Invented names only (Whitmore Road
station, The Lamplighter, Patel & Son, Sharp Lads, Viaduct Motors, Arch Seven Taproom, Bow Joinery). UK flags only.
