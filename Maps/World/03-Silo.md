# 03 Silo (World Tour, United States; large, about 247 x 173 m, 36,100 m2, 24 players)

Forested hills over a Cold War missile complex. Generated by `tools/world/silo.py` (run it from the repository root:
`python3 tools/world/silo.py . preview.png` rewrites the map and draws a top view of the surface and of the caves).

## Theme and layout

A long wooded ridge runs north to south down the middle of the map with the silo on its top: a shaft 23 m across, its
two concrete lids run back on their rails, a broken safety fence and floodlights round the rim. The hills either side
are covered in pine and broadleaf woods (about 300 trees, bushes, shrubs, leaf litter, boulders, fallen logs), with
small sandbag positions scattered through them (machine-gun nests, walled positions with ammunition, low walls under
camo nets). A chain-link perimeter fence with barbed wire and mine warning signs runs just inside the outline, which
rises into wooded hills (a cliff berm with scenery pines carrying the forest on past the edge).

Mirrored east to west (Blue west, Red east), with different theming on each side:

- **Blue: the motor pool** (a vehicle shed and a workshop, cargo trucks, jeeps, a fuel tanker, fuel and jerrycan racks).
- **Red: the main gate and headquarters** (the base fire station, the HQ office, a gatehouse with a boom gate, tank
  traps, staff cars, an armoured car, a helipad).
- Each side: a forward Bunker under the north hill, two concrete guard towers at the base corners, a portal into the
  hill (a concrete blast door portal at the end of a trench), two access bunkers (blockhouses with a stair down) and a
  vent shaft above the junction underground. Two more guard towers stand at the north and south ends of the ridge.

## Underground

All of it is terrain caves and holes (Maps/README.md "Holes and caves"); the rooms are buildings standing in cave
chambers at an absolute height (`"anchor": "world"`), so they keep their blast doors, furniture and nav waypoints:

| Level | Rooms |
|---|---|
| y = -4 | Launch Control Centre (north, on the axis: consoles, wall display, two-key launch station, computer rooms); the blast locks with decontamination behind each portal; Blue's crew quarters (mess, bunks, lockers, washroom) / Red's maintenance bay (workshop, fuel store, tool crib); the junctions under the vent shafts (ventilation fans, transformers); the lift lobbies; the silo's top gantry ring |
| y = -8 | Blue's armoury / Red's comms room (radio consoles, patch bays, crypto room) |
| y = -13 | the power plant (south, on the axis: reactor hall with a catwalk, switchgear, turbine hall); the silo's service-arm level |
| y = -22 | the silo floor, under the missile |

Tunnels (steel arch sets, strip lights and a concrete walkway, the rock darkening away from the openings) join them:
portal - blast lock - cableway - junction - silo; junction - crew quarters - launch control; junction - armoury (down
to -8) - power plant (down to -13); access bunkers to the side rooms; launch control and the power plant straight into
the silo at their levels. Two lifts run from lobbies off the launch control tunnel up their shafts to the hilltop
(players only: bots take the tunnels).

The silo: a service tower each side (switchback stairs, an opening onto the shaft on every level), the top gantry ring
(reached from the cableways and the launch control tunnel), service arms from the towers to a platform round the missile
at the power plant's level, and the floor. You can drop into the shaft from the rim (no fall damage), from any tower
level, or down a vent shaft into a junction.

## Game modes

- **King of the Hill:** launch control (y -4), the silo floor (y -22) and the reactor floor (y -13), all on the mirror
  line, all underground: the fight is for the rooms.
- **CTF / TDM:** spawns and flags in the two bases. The way across is over the hill (through the woods, the passes
  north and south, the silo pad) or under it (portal, blast lock, cableway, the silo's gantries), with the access
  bunkers and vent shafts as flanks between the two.
- **Free-for-all:** the base spawns, eight in the woods and nine underground (in the rooms of the complex).

The self-test ("World Tour: Silo") measures each team's nav walk to every hill and to the enemy flag (within 8%),
checks that every room of every building is reachable by bots, probes the tunnels and the silo levels, and that plants
grow over the buried rooms but never in them.

## Assets

- Buildings (general, in `Buildings/`): Launch Control Centre, Silo Power Plant, Silo Blast Lock, Silo Armoury, Silo
  Comms Room, Silo Crew Quarters, Silo Maintenance Bay, Silo Service Tower, Silo Access Bunker, Concrete Guard Tower,
  with `silo*` furnishing rules in `Buildings/Furnishing` (`tools/world/silo_buildings.py`; the Dev map **Silo
  Buildings** shows them).
- Props (general, `Props/Facility`, `tools/world/silo_props.py`): 27 control-room, power-plant, decon, armoury, comms,
  tunnel and yard props (consoles, wall displays, mainframes, reactor vessel, turbine, pumps, blast door portal and leaf,
  bulkhead lamps, cable trays, propellant tank, ventilation fan, guard booth, signs).
- Military props from `Props/Military` (sandbags, ammunition, vehicles, barbed wire).
- Decals: four of the map's own in `Maps/World/Decals/Silo` (floor trefoil, no-lone-zone marking, seepage streaks, a
  blast door stencil) and the global set (cracks, stains, leaf litter, tyre tracks, hazard stripes, stencil numbers).

## Costs

About 340 boxes drawn one by one (the tree trunks, 18 boulder cores, the silo's gantries, lids and missile core);
everything else is instanced: 26 buildings (about 3,300 shell boxes), about 600 hand-placed props plus 90 scattered
logs and stumps, about 1,600 meshes (the tunnel arch sets, the missile, the scenery forest), about 1,000 decals and
about 70,000 plants. The smoke run's `world_silo` (from over Blue's base), `world_silo_shaft` (down the shaft from the
top gantry) and `world_silo_control` (the launch control room) views log fps and draw calls.
