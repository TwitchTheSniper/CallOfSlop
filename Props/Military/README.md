# Military props

A reusable military library for any map: sandbags, wire, tank traps, guns, ammunition, pallets, camp gear, tanks, planes, cars and trucks. Original designs only (no real vehicle names, insignia or markings; the white "star" is an invented eight-pointed star). Generated by `tools/propviz/gen_military.py` (run `python3 tools/propviz/gen_military.py Props Maps/Dev/MilitaryProps.json` from the repository root; it rewrites this folder's JSON files and the Dev pack's **Military Props** map, which shows every one of them).

- `Props/Military/*.json`: category **Military** (36 props: 34 generated here, plus the hand-made **Landmine** and **Mine Warning Sign** from the landmines feature, see [../README.md](../README.md#landmines); the generator never rewrites those two but shows them on the gallery).
- `Props/Military/Vehicles/*.json`: category **Military Vehicles** (22 props; the file names the category, the folder only sorts them).

Clutter can scatter a whole category: `"props": ["Military"]`.

## Placing them

- Big vehicles and planes look best turned by multiples of 90 degrees: a solid prop at any other angle gets its collider cut into a few strips that follow the turned outline (Props/README.md), coarse for a 24 m wing.
- Props may now be up to **24 m** in any direction (was 12 m), so the planes are full size. `"scale"` on a `Prop` op still works (0.1 to 10).
- Tanks, the light tank, the jeep and the fighter's wings are low enough to mantle onto (1.7 m). The Guard Tower's platform is out of reach on purpose: put a `Block` or stairs beside it if a map wants a sniper perch.
- The Command Tent and the Transport Plane's hold are walk-in. Everything else big is closed cover.
- Barbed wire is cosmetic like the Beachhead pack's wire (players push through it, bullets pass): it reads as an obstacle, it doesn't trap anyone.
- Ammunition and fuel that explode follow the Volatile rules: a hit lights them, they spit sparks for the fuse time, then go off (and set off neighbours a moment later).

## Cost

Every prop is drawn instanced like the rest of the library (one mesh per prop type, a draw per type per 24 m chunk). Part counts: crates 5-36, guns 23-58, sandbag walls 50-118, tanks 68-97 (816-1,164 triangles), trucks 60-96, planes 72-104 (up to 1,248 triangles). Collider boxes: 1-2 per vehicle, 4-8 per plane, 1-6 elsewhere. The `Military props` self-test section logs the totals and the gallery's draws, and the smoke test looks at the gallery (`smoke_props_military_overview.bmp`, `smoke_props_military_tanks.bmp`).
