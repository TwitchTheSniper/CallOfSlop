# Rendering: what went wrong here before

Each line is a past failure's rule. Add a new one at the end: date, what went wrong, why, the rule.

- **Shared materials** (docs/render/MATERIALS.md). A part box holds a look (`BoxLooks`: colour, tone, material) and a material is a row of `Materials/Surfaces.json`: never store a material in a part, a save, a blueprint or the wire; a row's number is this build's own. Rows 0-6 are the old texture layers. A new shader that draws materials takes `Surfaces.Glsl` after `Lighting.Glsl` and calls `SurfaceLit`.
- **The back buffer's alpha is the bloom's mark** (docs/render/MATERIALS.md "Post effects"). Blended drawing in the world leaves it alone (`Renderer.BlendDefault`, not `BlendFunc`), and an opaque shader writes 1.
- **OpenGL 4.6 / 4.1** (docs/render/OPENGL46.md). The game asks for 4.6 core and falls back to 4.1 (always on macOS; `--gl41` forces it). Every shader source starts with `#version 410 core` and goes through `RenderCaps.Glsl`; 4.6-only GLSL sits in `#if GL46`; 4.6-only code checks a `RenderCaps` flag and keeps the 4.1 branch. Under xvfb llvmpipe gives 4.5, so set `MESA_GL_VERSION_OVERRIDE=4.6 MESA_GLSL_VERSION_OVERRIDE=460` for the 4.6 path. Dev builds log `[gl] error` lines with stack traces.
- **Baked lighting** (docs/render/LIGHTING.md). Every scene shader lights through `ApplyLightAt` and the `LitFrame` block (binding 7, texture units 12-14: don't reuse them).
- Per-frame state holding indices into a construct is stamped when taken and checked against the construct as it is when used (`ConstructRender.GhostLive`, `Program.LinkStamp`, `LinkStateCheck`, `LinkHitUsable`): a tick, a packet or the other peer can shift parts between input and draw, and a hit may name no part.
- "What is under the crosshair" on a moving thing uses the picture just drawn (`PanelRender.DrawnSpot`).
- Everything drawn together is sampled at one tick; when something looks wrong on a client, check the host alone first.
- Game code stamps tracers on `ConstructRender.Now`. Pool slots past the count are stale.
- 2026-11-10: from the shared materials to fix/zombie-far-horde no crowd zombie was drawn: the box FragmentSrc gained `vLocal`/`vLocalN`/`vSize` inputs, `Renderer.Crowd.cs` reuses it without writing them, the link failed once into the log and the F9 counters still counted the prepared instances. A shader that reuses another's fragment code is updated with its inputs in the same change (grep `FragmentSrc.Replace`), and a link failure logs the info log.
- 2026-11-12: a `"work": "smoke"` stack never smoked and a burning part showed no flames: `ConstructRender._fxDt` was only set in `FlamesBegin`, which nothing called, so every `Fx.Share(rate * _fxDt)` was 0; phase 1 shipped the tag without seeing a machine run. A visual effect is done when a `--window-shot` of the running machine shows it, and a per-frame value has a caller before it has users.
- 2026-11-12: a Conveyor Router's way lamps and fence never lit on the Production Showcase: a big body draws parts that never change from a cached batch (`ConstructRenderBatch.StaticBoxes`), and the Router's new box keys weren't in its list of moving or lit parts. A new kind of box drawn by state (lamp, rig, turn) also goes in `StaticBoxes`' list, and is checked on a big body, not a small test build.
- 2026-11-13: a made part's tile was drawn twice (4 of 64) and could keep a panel's unfinished picture: the catalogue atlas's remake (its key changes once after start-up) also cleared the made-part pool, which has pages of its own, and a tile was keyed to its def while a panel's facets arrive later by swapping `d.Boxes`. A cache of something drawn from a run-time def keeps through unrelated rebuilds and follows the def's boxes (`SandboxTiles.Made.cs`), not only the def.
- 2026-11-16: the Magpie over Crumb (2.4 km from the map's middle) shimmered and z-fought inside and out: the render origin only started at 4 km, so each part's world matrix rounded on its own and the camera cancelled kilometres, a millimetre of shake against the 2-5 mm face offsets; a rider's eye was also a world-space difference. Anything drawn against a moving body takes its origin from that body's drawn position, and the eye from it in small numbers (`Game.LocalSeatAnchor`, `LocalRideAnchor`); `Program.FarFromMiddle` stays small.
- 2026-11-18: every flame on the host burned by the construct's ThrustShow (a mouse turn lit the drive bells and every RCS jet), because ConstructRender's flame flag `_fxHost` was never set after the dt code moved into Draw and `FlamesBegin` lost its caller. When a per-frame setup moves, move every field it set; Draw sets `_fxHost` from blendTicks (IsServer).
- 2026-11-19: big deck plate and tread plate floors showed broad soft light and dark bands at low angles (a look review, high): the "tread" pattern was a full-cover sine rib 1/scale apart, which lit reads as shading stripes, not tread, and High's bump tilted the normal along a fixed world tangent, not the face's u and v. A pattern meant to show at a floor's scale is mostly flat with crisp filtered features that fade to their mean (`diamond`), and a bump's slope goes along the face's own frame from the derivatives; check a pattern change with `--gl-bench ... --bench-wall --bench-wall-finish NAME --bench-move 8` (shimmer) on Medium and High, and `--window-shot --materials High` low over a 16 m floor.
- 2026-11-20: the Mainframe's hull plates (2-5 mm apart) z-fought past about 100 m: the window's 24-bit depth steps 10 cm at 300 m with a 0.05 m near plane. The 4.6 path now draws the world with reversed 32-bit float depth (docs/render/DEPTH.md). A shader that writes clip depth itself uses `REVZ` (far is 0), polygon offsets go through `Renderer.PolygonOffset`, frustum near/far planes through `Renderer.DepthPlanes`, and code that rebinds the screen mid-frame binds `Renderer.MainFbo`.
- 2026-11-20: the rust bucket's Worn Flight Couch (fabric, weave) sparkled pixel by pixel on High on Caledon, 24 km from the middle (Sean): `SurfaceLit` built its bump frame from `dFdx(vWorld)`, and a world point that far out rounds to 2 mm, more than a pixel up close; the 2026-11-19 pattern bump started using that frame. Screen derivatives of a position come from small numbers (`vRel`, a prop's `vEye`), never the world point; try a material change on a planetoid map (`--window-shot --map Caledon --blueprint "Rust Bucket" --materials High --do "sit Worn Flight Couch#1"`), not only near the middle.
- 2026-11-21: on Karl's NVIDIA PC every post effect (bloom, haze, AO, contact shadows) was off for the whole run while the cloud's Mesa showed them: `GlDebug.Label` named VAOs and buffers that were only glGen'd (NVIDIA: GL_INVALID_VALUE, Mesa: fine), the error waited in the debug callback's pending flag, and PostFx's one-off back buffer check read it as its own. A one-off GL check clears pending errors right before the call it checks (or reads `glGetError` alone), and only bound objects get labels; when a Video switch "does nothing" on the PC, read the `[postfx]` and `[gl] error` lines in callofslop.log first.
- 2026-11-21: the Zero Day's sides went black outside and its rooms had hard lamp-shadow wedges (dark interiors PR 450, lamp shadows PR 455): `SunShadows.CasterOf` cast every box as its axis-aligned box, and a shaped box (a sloped `hull` plate, a wedge) fills a box much bigger than itself, so its own face sat inside its caster and saw neither sky, sun nor lamp. A caster, collider or occluder made from part boxes takes a shaped box's real shape (`CPartBox.ShapePoint` over `PartShapes.Mesh`), and a shadow change is tried on a ship with sloped hull plates (the Zero Day), outside in daylight as well as inside.
- 2026-11-22: the Zero Day works' 40 Hall Floodlights would never have lit near most of them: `ConstructLamps` and `PartAmbience` skipped a body whose origin was more than reach + 30 m from the eye ("a body's parts are within 30 m of its origin"), and the works is one 96 x 240 m body. A per-body distance cull goes by the body's own size (`WorldCom`, `Radius`), never a fixed guess.
- 2026-11-23: on the Zero Day works the still bodies' shadow map was never kept (PR 555's first bench: GPU up 2.5 ms): stillness was judged from the blended pose, which rounds differently every frame. Whether a body moved is read from its ticked pose (`Pos`/`Rot` against `PrevPos`/`PrevRot`), never from a blended one. A cache of a body's boxes is keyed to `ConstructShapeVersion`, not `RebuildStamp` (which bumps on any wire or setting); its versions are unique across worlds because body ids restart. A part skipped or put to sleep by a draw cull still makes its smoke, sparks and flames (`ConstructRender._fxEmitted` keeps it drawn).
- 2026-11-23: back-face culling on the merged construct pass (after winding 2,307 shapes outwards) gained ~0% on the works bench on the Windows PC and cut faces off a hex pipe fitting; the merged pass is vertex/draw bound, not fill bound. Measure a GPU idea on the PC before building on it; do not cull merged chunks.
- 2026-11-23: in the Zero Day works Sean saw a dark bubble round him that followed him, and light that flickered as he walked: the dark-interior maps covered only 24 m round the camera and faded to the open sky's light past it, and pendants 21 m up flipped between seen and hidden as their rays crossed roof steel (an 8 m jump in the lamp order, no fade). A screen-space or camera-centred light effect never fades to a different answer at its edge (`RoomShade`'s far ring), and a lamp's place in the order moves smoothly (`ConstructLamps.HideEase`); try lighting changes in a hall bigger than the maps, walking.
- 2026-11-23: the Zero Day works drew a 40 ms frame about once a second in Sean's world (never on the fresh bench): each Extruder step grows its piece in place (`RefreshShape`, `RebuildStamp++`), and every whole-body cache keyed to the stamp or to the part defs (merged plan, sun casters, the still shadow maps, lamp slots, lamps, sounds, contact foot) was made again for 5,500 parts. A piece growing on a die is drawn alone and left out of whole-body caches (`Construct.Growing`, `StructStamp`); a cache of every part of a body keys to `StructStamp` or `ConstructShapeVersion`, never `RebuildStamp`, and a big-build perf fix is tried with its machines running (`--station-check`), not only spawned.
- 2026-11-24: anchored bases never cleared planet plants: PlanetClearings' time gate began at long.MinValue and 'now - _at' overflowed negative, so it never ran. A first-time gate uses a flag, never a MinValue sentinel in a subtraction.
