# Constructs: what went wrong here before

Each line is a past failure's rule. Add a new one at the end: date, what went wrong, why, the rule. Drive lines, production machines, the network, physics and riding have files of their own beside this one, which load with their code.

- **Extrusions** (docs/sandbox/constructs/production.md "Extrusion"). A piece an Extruder, a Press or a Wheel Former makes is a `ConstructPartDef` made at run time, one per key (shape, section, size, length, material): its id (from `Extrusion.RuntimeBase`) is this machine's own and isn't in `ConstructParts.All`, so read a part's def through `CPart.D`, `DefOf` or `ConstructParts.Def`, never `All[id]`, and on the wire or in a hash use its `ExKey`, never its id.
- A part given to a player goes through `ConstructEdits.Give` (hands, backpack, drop).
- Try a part-onto-part feature through the player's whole chain (menu, PlaceNew, loose / frozen / mounted, the client's ghost, the Attach message, the server's result) for hand-made AND generated parts. Sweep a "won't attach" report over every receiver and category, not one pair.
- A "don't move into X" check refuses only moves that make a NEW overlap and skips what the body is joined to. Try held bodies off centre, on the floor and coupled.
- A visual change of a part's size also changes its collider, player blocking, ray hit, outline and ghost checks: solid shape goes through `Construct.SolidCount/SolidBox/ShapeBounds`.
- Scaled sockets apply recursively to every part fitted on a scaled part, so try merges with a part that has its own sockets (a splitter), not only a barrel.
- A fixed-pose (freeform) segment is rigid with its parent for every system: ask `Construct.MoveSeg/Rigid/FrameOf`, never `Seg != 0`. Compare link points in the rest frame (`ToRest`); sockets meeting across a seam are `SeamLinks`.
- Welder and wrench share the leaf code (`ConstructBuild.OnLeaf/LeafRefusal/HangOnLeaf`). Try a weld path with every role whose behaviour depends on its links (wheels, suspensions, joints, drive line), driven, not only posed.
- A part's behaviour is gated on its own inputs and links, never on the construct being a vehicle (a seat, free, awake). An optimisation that skips idle bodies wakes them on an input or power change (`ConstructPhysics.EngineLive`).
- **Blueprints and bodies.** A save records what is: steps' `"at"`, links' `"laid"`/`"hosts"`, `"exactLinks"`, a step's `"also"`, points relative to step 0. Round trips compare parts, joints, links and bodies, on selections of real vehicles too.
- Carrying parts across bodies remaps every joint index (`Hinge`, `Child`) and moves only what sits in the body frame (`FrameOf(seg) == 0`); try a join with a turned welded piece and read it back as a client.
- Points on a part go through `SegToWorld`; reach is one helper per action shared by client and server (`AttachReach`, `WeldReach`, `ReachPoint`).
- 2026-11-10: every headless run failed or hung in `World`'s static initializer: a shell gun built while the blueprints loaded read `World.GravityScale`, the initializer loaded the maps, a sandbox map looked up a blueprint and re-entered the blueprints' Lazy. A static initializer never loads data that can lead back to its caller: take it on first use (`World.Map`).
- 2026-11-10: event doors (plain "hang" socket, role door) had no usable snap point, since SnapBuild made every door single-purpose by its hinge leaf. A part family with a new way to attach is classified in `SnapBuild.Build` in the same change.
- 2026-11-11: a new CfgKey with "options" was refused ("options belong to a choice") and its part dropped, which also dropped its recipes and blueprints; `--check-construct-parts` prints to callofslop.log, not stdout, so the refusal went unseen. A new choice key goes in `ConstructConfig.DefaultType` (or its part file says `"type": "choice"`); read the REFUSED lines in the log, not the console.
- 2026-11-11: every working production machine drew 0 W in survival: a Data out set `Monitor`, which made `HasOutput` and so `IsSignal` true, and `ConstructPower.Step` counts a signal part's draw only while its `Out` is on. A flag that changes what a part is (`HasOutput`, `IsSignal`, roles) is checked against every reader of it (`grep -n "\.IsSignal"`).
- 2026-11-12: main's full suite went red with a day's production merges, all on branches that ran only their own sections (a screen became its line state; machines gained a Data out; tier cylinders made 52 pieces; the tile atlas gained materials after the tools; round wheels have no hull). A change to what a part is, shows or holds runs the live sections that name that kind of part by name before merging (`--selftest-list | grep`).
- 2026-11-12: a pick from a machine's own Recipe button stayed on the host (`OwnRecipe` returned before `StructureDirty` was marked). Any server path that changes a part's setting marks its body dirty; try a setting change once on a joined client.
- 2026-11-13: no glass sheet could be formed (and so no pane's blank): `PanelNumbers` clamped armour with `Math.Clamp(x, 1, MaxArmour)`, and glass's MaxArmour is 0, so the clamp threw. A clamp whose bounds come from a data table guards min over max (or skips the clamp), and a new material is tried through `--form-panel` once.
- 2026-11-16: "Mods" failed once the first shipped blueprint with sub-blueprint uses came (the Submodule Skiff): those are flattened after every shipped one is read and were appended, so a mod's new blueprint was no longer last. A load pass that defers some entries puts them back where they were read (`ConstructBuild.LoadBlueprints`'s `usesLater` index), and a shipped-list change runs "Mods" by name.
- 2026-11-17: Turret Ring S/M/L would have turned "Joint sides" red: its hidden race and trunk boxes had no side, its turning plate shared the trunk's colour and was paintable. A new joint part tags every box (hidden ones too) base or leaf, gives the leaf boxes a colour no base box has and `"paintable": false`, and runs `--selftest-only "Joint sides, Joint welds"` (the ring also turned "Joint welds" red on PR 252: its trunk goes down through a deck, so it can't sit on that rig's frame block; works mounts are left out of it).
- 2026-11-17: a joined client's gun said "wire a signal in to fire" though wired: `CPart.InCount` is counted by the logic tick, which only the server runs. A look line that asks whether a part is wired reads `ConstructLogic.WiredIn` (the copy's wires and cross wires), never `InCount` alone.
- 2026-11-17: the Zero Day's aft ladder said "no room at the top" (a Fire Director put on its top landing), as did the Magpie's (Shell Control Chairs) and the Dray's (its hatch 0.2 m from the hold's wall): nothing tried a climb after fittings moved. A fitting placed near a hatch or a ladder, or a hatch moved, runs `--ladder-check` (every shipped ladder climbed up and down, naming what is in the way).
- 2026-11-18: the Zero Day's magazines never launched (Space, buttons, crew): a launch's way-out ray hit the torpedo bay's convex collider, which closes the port its look leaves open, and nothing ran a launch after the bays were made. A launch ray passes the rack's own mount (`ConstructOrdnance.Blocked`); a rack in a shaped part is tried by firing it, not by reading its wires.
- 2026-11-18: locked Zero Day torpedoes fired in the start's orbit never hit the Traders' ship 400 m behind: they turned their speed over the planet (200 m/s of orbit) and so circled, their motor pushed them on down towards the ground, and the orbit (7% air) wasn't space, so they coasted 6 s. A guided weapon steers and pushes by its speed against its target (`OrdnanceFlight.ChaseTurn`, `SpaceBurn`), and a weapon is tried in a ship start's own orbit against a ship there, locked, not on a test range.
- 2026-11-18: Zero Day torpedoes fired facing away from their locked target turned back at it from the first tick (reaction jets turn anywhere) and fell back through the ship like dropped bombs. A missile off a rack or rail runs straight out on its motor until it is clear of its own craft before its seeker steers (`OrdnanceFlight.BoostOff`); a launch is tried with the target behind the ship as well as ahead.
- 2026-11-18: boarded crews fired 300+ rounds a run into their own ship's walls and deck: the bots' `BotSees` reads `World.Query` only (no constructs), and `BotAim` aims along the planet's up, wrong on a rolled ship or in space. Anything that judges sight or aims inside a construct checks the line with `SandboxRay` and works along the rider's own up (`Player.Chest`, `RideUpW`): `Game.CanSeeBoarder`, `GuardAim`.
- 2026-11-18: the Dray's leg pods lowered on the ground left it on its belly with its feet buried: a held door's collider moves only when it settles, and a foot that appears 2 m under the ground is past `MaxGroundDepth` (1 m), so no contact lifted it (a settled door also left a parked body asleep). A door whose collider moves wakes its body (`ConstructDoors.Settle`), and a leg that comes down on the ground lifts its craft as it is drawn (`GearLift`); try gear and anything that extends a collider with the body standing on the ground, not only in the gallery.
- 2026-11-20: a flush pod's host search found no surface on a freeform-welded floor: a freeform weld's part has its `Pos` in its own segment's frame, so `PartBounds` of parts on different segments can't be compared directly. Compare in the body's rest frame (`RestPose` of each part's segment), or within one `FrameOf` as `ConstructBuild.Blocked` does, and never with the live `SegPose` angle for a placing check.
- 2026-11-21: F refused every module on a socket a player welded onto a craft ("a socket on a joint takes its module by the attach tool's socket weld"): a face-point or off-grid socket weld puts the plate on a fixed freeform segment, and `ModuleOn` refused any `Seg != 0`, then posed the module from the plate's raw numbers (its weld's frame). Gate on `Rigid`, read a plate through `ModuleSockets.PlateRest`, and merge a module into its plate's weld frame (`PlanOnPlate`).
- 2026-11-21: a Flush or Well socket a player welded onto a deck never dug its well (no hole, and F then said the deck was in the socket's room): `ModuleCut.PlanOf` refused any plate with `FrameOf(seg) != 0` as "on a joint", and its frame read the plate's numbers as the body's. Same rule as the line above: gate on `Rigid` and place a plate through its segment's frame (`WorldFrame`, `PlateRest`).
- 2026-11-21: the Building Station was refused once the Dress style setting came ("options (2 to 16 names) belong to a choice"): DressStyles.json holds one style, so the choice had one option. A choice built from a data table is added only when the table has two entries or more, and its reader clamps the index.
- 2026-11-22: a Stow Drawer in a module fitted on a welded socket said "F: open or shut" and F gave "nothing to use there" (Sean): `BuildDoors` took only door parts with `Seg == 0`, and a module on a freeform-welded plate merges into that weld's segment (`PlanOnPlate`), so its drawers and doors were never event doors. Event doors take any `Rigid` segment and work in its frame (`ToRest`, `RestAabb`, the drawn pose times `SegMatrix`); a module feature is tried on a socket welded freeform, not only on one built on the grid.
- 2026-11-22: a Flush socket's well on a Large Timber Deck showed no sides (Sean): each re-cut at a new depth added a hole and a seal-sleeve liner to the deck's holed key, the next liner pushed the key past `MaxRuntimeName` and was refused (logged only). A well's liner is three numbers on its hole's entry (`Hole.WithLining`), and a hole down the same line replaces the old one; anything added to a run-time key is measured against its length with several on one part.
- 2026-11-22: a ladder chained through a dock found no docking hatch by its top once the hatch had slid open: a settled slide door's part `Pos` moves by its travel. Anything that asks where a door is for a door it may have settled open reads `ConstructDoors.ShutPos`, and a door list is read after `EnsureDoors` (it is built lazily, `DoorCount` is 0 before).
