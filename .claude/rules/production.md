# Production machines, power, logic and links: what went wrong here before

Each line is a past failure's rule. Add a new one at the end: date, what went wrong, why, the rule.

- **Run-time pieces.** What an Extruder, a Press or a Wheel Former makes is a def made at run time whose id is this machine's own: read it through `CPart.D`, `DefOf` or `ConstructParts.Def`, never `ConstructParts.All[id]`; on the wire use `ConstructStorage.WriteDef`/`ReadDef` (its key), never `U16(id)`; in a hash use `ExKey`.
- Most of `Constructs/Parts`, `Assembling.json` and `ModularEngine.json` are the part kit's output: change the generator (`Source/PartKit`), and cap every amount by the file's limits (one recipe over `MaxUnits` makes the whole Assembling table refuse).
- A machine's draw is decided by its store (Busy, Held), never by its signal output (2026-11-11: every working machine drew 0 W once a Data out made it a signal part).
- 2026-11-11: conveyor riders froze on a client 1 s after the server's last word, and a second line on one body put riders out of their link's group (appended, while the step reads them grouped). Anything a client draws moving between messages is stepped on the client by the server's rule; a list read in groups is kept grouped where it is added to.
- 2026-11-11: the Production Showcase ticked at 8-60 ms twice a second while its Wheel Former grew: a growing wheel is a new def every 5 cm, and `ConstructHulls.Of` made a convex hull of its lathe (6 ms, about 60 KB) for each, inside the 296-part body's collider rebuild. A run-time def made per growth step sets `ColliderBoxes` (hidden boxes, no hull) as a growing round does; time a tick spike down to the per-part call (stopwatch per part in `ConstructCollider.Build`) before guessing at its source.
- 2026-11-11: auto-routed links, conveyors worst, arrived with two to four short right-angle jogs: the lattice search may only turn after a run of 1.5 radii and arrives on a 10 cm stub, so an offset of a few cm became a detour. A router judges its result, not only its cost: re-solve the ends in at most two turns, size stubs and runs by the link's width, keep hugging a tie-breaker, and measure with `--route-ends`.
- 2026-11-12: a Conveyor Router's Alarm output read 0 while a piece was stuck: the logic tick sets every non-logic part's next output to 0 unless it is passed through (`ConstructLogic`: the Extruder's Length, the Router's Alarm). A machine that sets `Out` itself also gets a pass-through line in the logic tick, and its output is checked on a wire, not only on the part.
- 2026-11-12: a Router's way lamps never lit on the Production Showcase: a big body draws parts that never change from a cached batch (`ConstructRenderBatch.StaticBoxes`). A new kind of box drawn by state also goes in `StaticBoxes`' list, and is checked on a big body.
- 2026-11-12: the Panel Former's screen couldn't be touched: its board was a look box, which the crosshair's ray passes. A screen with buttons is mounted on a solid box.
- 2026-11-12: a world loaded with every main switch off: `ReadBody` read each part's State, then called `ConstructConfig.Init`, whose `StartState` sets a switch from its Start on setting. A loader that calls an initialiser after reading saved fields keeps the saved ones, and a save is tried in a fresh process.
- Before merging a machine change, run the live sections that name that kind of machine (`--selftest-list | grep`): a screen that became its line state, a new Data out, a new tile after the resources each broke a neighbour's section on main (2026-11-12).
- 2026-11-13: arms never lifted a press's output (lifters phase 4 marked it done): `MadeBy` asked the press's rows, which only a lathe has since presses went to sized dies. A check of what a machine makes asks its current path (`SizedDieOf`: presets and `Pressed` keys) and is tried with the machine running, not read.
- 2026-11-13: a Building Station's build with an Ammo Container never finished: `ConstructConveyor.RackCheck` refused every storage part for every rack, the station's hold included, so the order's part could never arrive and the window tool's stock was refused. A new kind of part a build may need is tried through each way into the station's hold (an order, a hand, a stock), not only the parts the showcase builds.
- 2026-11-13: a small pressed receiver weighed (and recycled for) more than its blank and Gear Sets: the merge's mass rule had no cap. A pressed piece's mass never passes `Pressed.CostMass`; `--press-part sweep` reports one that does.
- 2026-11-13: a Lay-down Pad's take-in crashed the server on a loose Swept Wing (lifters phase 5's "as new" test called `ConstructFluid.Held`, which reads a Fluid Tank's own setting slots: index -1 on any other part). A helper that reads one role's settings is called only for that role; try a check that loops over loose parts with an odd part (a wing, a beam), not only the piece it was written for.
- 2026-11-13: a chained order sent a third blank through a Loader Arm when two were asked: a piece that reached a lifter's pick rack has lost its rider's tag, so the planner's `Riding` stopped counting it and sent another. Whatever carries a piece on after a store (an arm, later a crane) counts as riding for the order it serves (`ConstructLifters.AtPicks`).
- 2026-11-13: a world loaded mid-build ordered parts already on the station's pick pad: the station's first order pass ran before the lifters' scan (no arm, no pad known yet), and arms loaded with a piece are Stuck, which the pad count skipped. A count that depends on another system's scan makes it run first (`PickPadHolds` scans), and a state a load gives on purpose (`LiftWhy.Holding`) isn't read as a fault.
- 2026-11-13: the Production Showcase's first Laser Cutter never got its blank: the conveyor from the Router into the cutter was 0.6 m and the blank 1 m, so the Router read "the conveyor after the junction is full" and sent the blank to overflow. A conveyor after a junction or into a machine is at least as long as the longest piece that rides it; read `logi why` on the Router when an ordered piece goes to storage.
- 2026-11-13: an unordered Ammo Container stuck at a Router blocked its line for good, and an ordered one was turned from a terminal's tray as "full": `RackCheck` refuses store parts and both callers read any refusal as "no room". A refusal code is read by its meaning (full waits; never-fits goes elsewhere or off the line), and a piece with no destination never blocks a line (`ConstructLogistics.NoPlace`).
- 2026-11-14: a terminal's order served by a Loader Arm never filled (lifters phase 5b marked it done): since sized parts phase 6 a press puts a preset part into its own output tray, and the arm only lifted loose pieces at a machine's out. When a machine's output path changes (tray, floor, conveyor), every taker of its output is tried again with the machine running (the arm now lifts from the tray too).
- 2026-11-14: a bored engine weighed twice a plain one: a pressed piece weighed its whole blank (a Small Cylinder's preset 77 kg against the file's 28), so anything off a preset was about three times its catalogue neighbour, and the kit's bore scaled the wall up too. A sized part's mass is continuous with its catalogue presets (`Pressed.PieceMass` for engine pieces), and a changed number keeps its value at the plain size (health stays the blank's: saves hold it absolute); press a key one step off a preset and compare it with the preset's part.
- 2026-11-14: a 5 x 5 cm Extruder order was taken and then stalled for good as "too many different pieces in this game": `WorkOrders.Refusal` checked the die's top and the length but not `Extrusion.MinSectionCm`, so `Extrusion.Def` returned null every step. An order check refuses everything the maker's `Valid` refuses (both ends of every range), and a stall's fallback text names the real cause.
- 2026-11-14: a Wheel ordered of an Assembler whose own Piece out loops back to it never started: the chain never asked the machine that needs an input (`FindMaker` skipped `mk == e.Dest`), and the starved Wheel line held the head. A machine may serve its own input when its out reaches its in, its line first (`AheadOfUsers`); try a chain on one machine as well as on two.
- 2026-11-14: a crane lowered a 2 m plate through a player standing on a pad (lifters phase 3 and 6 marked "waits for a player" done): the push returned at once for a player in a construct's ride frame, which is anyone standing on a construct (a floor slab, a pad, a prop). A check that skips players in a ride frame says what happens to them instead (`ConstructLifterPush.PinRiders`: the lifter waits), and a push is tried with the player standing on a construct, not only on the ground.
- 2026-11-14: a crane on the Production Showcase walked its rails again on every cut of an Extruder on its body (26 walks in 40 s): the rails' key hashed the body's part count, which a growing piece changes. A key of what a cached result depends on hashes only that (`CraneKeyOf`), and is tried on a body with a machine running.
- 2026-11-14: a press's brackets never left its out while an Extruder beside it kept making angles: an idle lifter's look tried its out points in the same order every time, and the first always had a piece the press wanted. A lifter's outs take turns (`CLift.OutTurn`); a line is watched for a minute with every machine running, not one piece at a time.
- 2026-11-14: a cut panel ordered on the Production Showcase was never cut, and its Panel Former made blank after blank into a Long Piece Rack: the chain tags a blank for the cutter "Router or not" (`ConstructLogistics.AtSource`), a plain conveyor ended at a rack with no way on, the rack took it, `Arrived` ignored a piece in a store that is not its link's, and `Step` asked the finished maker again. A link counts what lies on its network with no way on (`Serve`, `Lying`: from what stores hold, never a claim, since claims are not saved) and its row says why it waits, from the ask on (`EndsShort`); try a chain whose maker has no conveyor way to the machine, count what the maker made, and save and load mid-order.
- 2026-11-14: an order at the Production Showcase's designer sat at "0/1 from the Panel Former" with its line console stopped, and a Laser Cutter with no conveyor in said only "needs X on its bed" to a player holding the blank: neither said what the player must do. A row or look line that waits names the act that ends the wait (`Note`: "held off: press Start on its line console"; `ConstructLaserCutter.Lacks`: only a conveyor brings it); a client's copy of a row keeps the writer's 255 (`r.Text(255)`), or a second reason is cut off.
- 2026-11-14: a Lifter Cab's hook went 0.38 m into a cave's rising floor when moved across, then the next Ctrl popped it up: the hook's floor ran only while lowering (`move.Y < 0`). A floor rule for a moving thing runs on every move, and is tried on a bowl or a slope, not a flat test ground.
- 2026-11-14: the Factory Showcase's station asked its sheet Extruder for a chassis bar, which rode into the Panel Former and could never arrive, while the blank Extruder with a road to the station stood idle: a terminal's direct ask (`ConstructCrafting.StepTerminal`) took the first linked machine that accepted, and `LooseRefusal` only caught a machine with no conveyor out at all. A terminal's or station's ask tries the machines with a way to it (`RouteTo`) first, as chains rank them; lay a second maker of the same material when you test.
- 2026-11-14: an Extruder on a started line ran its own settings (a 25 cm bar every 2 m) until the first order came, filling racks and blocking Routers: only a queue, even an empty one, makes a machine orders-only, and a blueprint couldn't give one. A factory blueprint marks its makers `"ordersOnly": true` (`BlueprintScript.OrdersOnly`, `ConstructBuild.SetConfig`).
- 2026-11-14: the Factory Showcase's Gear and Cable presses stalled "Tray full" for good: their trays chuted straight into an Assembler, which takes a part only while its current recipe needs it. A press's tray chutes into a store (a Parts Shelf), and machines draw from the store.
- 2026-11-14: the Factory Showcase's Cable press made 197 Copper Coils for an order of two: a tray part reached its Assembler by chute through a store, which the chain never counted as arrived, so it asked again. A press with a Piece out conveyor now sends every part on it (`ConstructPress.PutOut`), tagged for its order; a maker feeding a machine for a chain delivers by conveyor, not through a store.
- 2026-11-14: an Assembler stalled with a Swing Arm at its head, needing a Bearing that only it made (its hopper takes only the head recipe's inputs), after its other orders used the Bearings made for the arm. Inputs pooled in one machine's hopper aren't held for the order that asked for them; not fixed (planner, `ConstructLogisticsPlan`), handed over.
- 2026-11-14: the Factory Showcase's Assembler stopped for good with a Copper Coil (for a later Servo Motor) at the front of its one input conveyor, refused because the head order (a Differential) didn't use it, and the Differential's Gear Sets queued behind it. A conveyor's delivery to an Assembler takes a part any of its queued orders uses (`FeedQueuedPart`); a machine fed by one conveyor is tried with two orders whose inputs arrive in the other's order.
- 2026-11-14: with every lacking part ordered at once (the user's ask), a Building Station's hold filled with parts whose parents weren't in, and the next part to fit waited at its door for good ("the tray is full"). A delivery the build can fit now makes room: one part no step can take yet moves to a rack with a road back (`ConstructStation.MakeRoomFor`); a change to when parts are ordered is tried with a hold that fills.
- 2026-11-14: with several orders queued at one Assembler (a station orders everything at once), each order's inputs were asked short: `Need` counted the hopper's parts and every chain riding to the machine, another order's included, as its own. An order counts only its own chains, and the hopper only when nothing is queued ahead of it; try a chain with two orders at one machine that share an input.
- 2026-11-14: ordering every lacking part at once could fill a Building Station's hold with parts whose parents weren't in, the next part to fit kept in a Router's storage for good (code review). A station orders a step only when its parent is in or its parent's part is held or asked (`StepComing`), makes room for the next part itself (`MakeRoomFor`, one decision with a fit check, spares set aside and not re-ordered), and says when it is stuck; a change to when parts are ordered is tried with a hold filled by `station stock`.
- 2026-11-14: a Building Station ordered children once their parent was merely ordered, so within depth x 0.5 s the whole tree was ordered and the hold filled again; a hand-taken part was ordered again at once; a part that could never fit moved 8 spares out and back each look (code review 2). A station orders a part only when what it goes onto is fitted, counts a hand-taken part while a player is near, refuses a never-fitting part once with the reason, and tries making room once per change of the hold; a rule a player relies on is one sentence they can predict.
- 2026-11-14: ordering only parts whose parent was fitted could not stall but took the Runabout from 4 to 12 minutes (code review 2). The user chose "order ahead, keep room": ready parts at once, one level ahead while a dry-run packing of the hold (everything held and coming) still fits the largest ready part (`OrderAhead`, `SimHold`): 7.25 minutes. A rule that trades speed for safety is put to the user with its numbers.
- 2026-11-16: a Rust Bucket corridor lamp wire crossed 1.5 m of open air under the ceiling (pre-built conduit, PR #226): a metre of air cost 6 against a wall pierce's 2.5 and a bend's 0.8, so the cut across won, and the first wire's run drew the next ones beside it. A ship's conduit tries air at `ConstructConduit.AirShip` first; measure a router change with `--conduit-check` on every ship (its "in the open" column), and bump `RouterVersion` so the disk cache lays them again.
- 2026-11-16: the Halberd's and Zero Day's turret and hatch wires still crossed their bays after air went to 30 a metre: with air dear, the plain guess (the straight distance at the hugging price) is weak, so the weighted search wandered, ran out of time or settled for a route 1.5 times the best. A dear-air search judges its route by what it measures (`OpenLength`) and searches again with the true cost to the goal as its guess (`RouteAsk.Exact`); a guess that costs a pass over the lattice is kept for the routes that need it (it doubled the load's searching when used for every route).
- 2026-11-16: linked logistics' merge review found IsStore's new clause with a `//` comment in the middle of the expression: everything after it on that line (`!IsAssembler && !IsRefiner && ... && !IsGunBench`) was commented out, so terminals counted machine holds as stock, and it built. A comment inside a multi-line boolean expression goes at the end of its last line, never mid-line; read the joined expression back after an edit.
- 2026-11-17: the factory kit's refinery heaps and loading bay (sized Storage Volumes standing in the open) spilt Material Bundles once past a quarter full: an open face's lowest corner under the level pours (ConstructSeal, Storage margin 0.25), and only the floor sealed them. Stock in the open goes in a bin (the factory_shell Ore Bunkers), or a Storage Volume walled on its sides; a check that feeds a store counts the bodies that appear.
- 2026-11-17: the Mining Shell's recipe was put after the Robot Shell's in Assembling.json, which would have shifted every later recipe of the plain Assembler's family in saved worlds (an old Assembler on Shell Control Chair would make Mining Shells): Assembling recipe choices are saved by index within their machine's family, so a new recipe row only ever goes at the end (`Assembling.LateHand`, `LateFolders`).
