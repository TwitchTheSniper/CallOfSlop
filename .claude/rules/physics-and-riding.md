# Physics, riding and flight: what went wrong here before

Each line is a past failure's rule. Add a new one at the end: date, what went wrong, why, the rule.

- For a solver instability, print each body's position, tilt and speed by tick before changing a number, change one thing at a time, and read anything a contact needs from the tick's velocities before the first impulse.
- When a physics change breaks a scene, first check whether the new result is what the shape should do; a scene puts a body where a real shape would move or fall.
- A visual change of a part's size also changes its collider, player blocking, ray hit, outline and ghost checks: solid shape goes through `Construct.SolidCount/SolidBox/ShapeBounds`.
- A "don't move into X" check refuses only moves that make a NEW overlap and skips what the body is joined to.
- Per-tick work against the world is bounded per body whatever its size (2026-11-10: a Titan Walker lying on Caledon made 5,300 ground queries a tick); measure with the biggest blueprint lying on a slope, not standing on the flat.
- A run-time def made per growth step sets `ColliderBoxes`, not a hull (2026-11-11: a growing wheel built a 6 ms convex hull every 5 cm).
- A position fix that undoes a move along up goes back along that same up (`GravityFrames.UpAt`), never along a world axis (2026-11-12: players standing still on a tilted planet floor drifted 1-10 cm/s); try a change to standing with a player left still for 6 s on a tilted planet floor.
- **Riding.** Every way of leaving a moving thing is a ride-frame path (`ConstructRide.End`, or off with its surface velocity); state that decides how a replay runs arrives in the same message as the move.
- A world-side check on a construct is judged as the frame's mover would (any face up, a capsule on an edge); entry tests drop onto edges, beams and turned hulls, size tests use the biggest builds (`RideTall`).
- "What is under the crosshair" on a moving thing uses the picture just drawn (`PanelRender.DrawnSpot`).
- A view that replaces another has its own remembered mode and is tried with the other modes on.
- In flight, along the ground is `Along(v, up)` and up is `BodyUp`; try flight changes away from the world's +Y side.
- 2026-11-11: after the survival physics gun went to 5,000 kg, "physics gun feel" failed: its 450 kg "heavy" piece was no longer limited by the beam. A check that reads a limit takes its amounts from the constant (`SurvivalMaxMass * 0.3f`), not a number picked against the old value.
- 2026-11-13: a watcher's copy of a rider stepped back 5.4 cm: past their newest snapshot a remote player was carried on at the snapshot's velocity, but a rider who has stepped off a moving seat slows in the air. Anything drawn ahead of its newest network sample uses the motion the samples show (`Game.SampleSnapshots`).
- 2026-11-14: no nav link on a planetoid ever climbed a stair: `World.CanWalkCurved` stepped its samples with `World.Slerp(ua, ub, f)`, and for two ups a few metres apart the dot rounds to 1 in floats, so Slerp returned the first up and every sample was taken over the link's start. Interpolate nearby directions with a normalised lerp, never an acos of their dot; `World.Slerp` still does this (GravityFrames' smoothing, ConstructThrust).
- 2026-11-14: a rider standing in a ship in orbit was drawn with the legs torn away: the leg animator kept planted feet and the ground's height in world coordinates, and the construct moved 300 m/s under them. Anything a rider's drawing keeps from frame to frame lives in the rider's frame (`Player.RenderRideFeet`), never the world's.
- 2026-11-16: an ordered ship on Ship keys sat on the ground burning fuel, and a hovering one flew about its point at full speed: the Ship pattern stopped dead whenever the autopilot was on (it never read the height or the point it held), and on a planetoid the autopilot measured the way to its point whole, height included, so a craft 1 km over its point was "1 km away". A new key pattern is tried with the autopilot on in each mode, and a distance over curved ground is taken at the craft's own height (`ConstructFlight` "The way it goes").
- 2026-11-16: things made aboard a moving ship were left behind by every path but the five f4c955791 covered (machines' outputs, pastes, loose parts, wrecks), and riders crossing onto a lift or a ramp switched frames (a drawn pop: snapshots interpolate within one `RideBody`/`RideSeg`). A new body's motion comes from one helper (`ConstructWorld.Create` marks it fresh, `ConstructRide.SpawnIntoMotion` gives it its frame's before the physics steps), and a player's frame is the construct's body, with a moving segment only carrying them in it (`MoveState.RideOn`); try with `--ride-check`.
- 2026-11-17: parts detached from a fast ship still flew off behind it, though every path gave them the ship's velocity: the physics' free-body speed limit (60 m/s in air, 2,000 in thin air) cut that speed on their first step (a ship flies faster on its surfaces or out of the bubble). A body that comes into the world moving keeps that speed past the limit, slowing but never speeding up past it (`Construct.SpeedKept`, set in `ConstructRide.SpawnIntoMotion`); `--ride-check --speed 120` steps one cut-free piece to show it.
- 2026-11-17: the Zero Day's docked Shuttle was drawn metres off its cradle in orbit: the host draws a body between `PrevPos` and `Pos`, a ship posed out of the bubble has both the same, but `ConstructCargo.Follow` moved only the carried body's `Pos`, so it lagged a tick (13 m at 800 m/s). Anything that poses one body from another sets its last pose from the other's last pose too.
- 2026-11-17: the Zero Day let its Shuttle go in orbit at 258 m/s and it came out at 52, falling: the contact solver read a non-dynamic body's velocity as zero, and a ship out of the bubble is posed (not dynamic) though it moves, so the clamp the Shuttle still touched braked it; a let-go body (not fresh) was also held to the free-body speed limit. A posed body that moves gives the solver its velocity (`VelSide`, `SpaceShip != 0`), and every path that sets an existing body's velocity from a moving frame calls `Construct.KeepSpeed` (cargo release, a bay's let-go, the physics gun aboard).
- 2026-11-17: after that fix the Shuttle shook on the host (a client was smooth): ships out of the bubble are posed in `ServerTickSpace`, which runs after the constructs' step, so the cargo followed last tick's pose and was drawn up to a tick off as the ship moved on. What is posed from a body follows it after the body's last move of the tick (`ConstructCargo.FollowPosed` after the space step); check the order in `Game.ServerTickStep`, not the comments.
- 2026-11-17: on a joined client a held piece was glued to the crosshair (no swing or lag, which the host has): the holder drew the aim pose plus a correction, never the beam's spring. Anything a client predicts that the server simulates is stepped on the client by the server's own rule (`ConstructGrab.Follow`, on the mover clock) and eased towards the server's pose of the same tick (GameSandboxPhysGunSim.cs); try it with `swing` in two windows with `--lag`.
- 2026-11-18: the Zero Day turned on its 2.25 MN drive bells and retros, so with its dampeners off every mouse turn lit the main engines and Karl read the keys as mapped wrong: the stabilizer's mix took the least sum of squared throttles, which leans on the strongest jets for every row. On Ship keys the first share is by squared force (`ConstructFlight.Shares`, `FlightEffectors.Lean`), the make-up rounds as before; a mix change is tried with `--ship-keys-check` (each key's mean jet levels) and `--flight-check` on every ship, since the plain weighted share alone left them sinking when let go.
- 2026-11-18: on the host, standing in the Limpet docked in a Zero Day in orbit, the look jumped between the couch, the floor and the frigate's keel (or started inside the keel): `RideDrawnPose` put only the ridden body at its drawn pose, and the carrier moving with it stayed at the tick's, up to a tick's travel (3.5 m at 208 m/s) off. Whatever moves rigidly with the ridden body (its carrier and the carrier's cargo) is posed with it; try aim changes from a docked craft in the Zero Day start (`--survival --start ZeroDay --map Caledon`, `sit`, `getout`, `aim`).
- 2026-11-18: a Shuttle told to dock with the Zero Day in orbit, and the Zero Day, spun for good and clipped into each other: the clamp's tractor ran on ships out of the bubble, whose poses come from their flights, so each tick its pull on the Shuttle was undone while its spin (kept by a ship on Ship keys) and its push back on the frigate stayed; a ship the clamp's box caught there also stayed in its flight, posed by both. Nothing that moves a body by its velocity works on a ship out of the bubble (`SpaceShip != 0`): change its flight's state (`SpaceShips.SetState`) or take it out of the flight first.
- 2026-11-18: a Land order brought the Shuttle down tipped 22 deg all the way to the ground: on the Ship pattern the autopilot with nobody steering held the pitch rate at zero, so whatever pitch the space descent left stayed, and only the roll came level. An autopilot in a sky levels the nose too (`ConstructFlight` Ship case), and a landing waits near the ground until it is level; try a landing order from space (`hoist 20; order land 3000; nav` shows the tilt).
- 2026-11-18: parts taken off inside a ship in orbit still fell out of it (after PRs 269 and 278): past the edge a loose body became a ship of its own, coasting apart from the ship it was in, and two posed ships never collide, so the frigate's steering ran its walls through the piece. A loose body inside a ship in space stays a body of the bubble, weightless and carried by that ship's motion each tick (`Game.SpaceRideFind`/`SpaceCarry`, `Construct.SpaceRide`), and whether it is still inside is asked where the carry puts it (at a raised time speed the ship is posed far on first). Try a change here at 1x, 10x and 100x.
- 2026-11-18: the Zero Day's Shuttle flew through other ships in orbit (and one Zero Day through another, 2 m in and on): each ship out of the bubble is posed by its flight, so the step never pairs two of them. Posed ships are paired after they are all posed (`Game.SpaceCollide`, `ConstructPhysics.ShipContact`) and kept apart by changing their flights' states; try ships meeting in space with the window tool's `ram N SPEED GAP [ID]` and `gapto N [ID]`.
- 2026-11-18: a Zero Day on a Dock order came down a station's lane 53 degrees off its berth and the clamp arm turned it in sideways: `SpacePose` levelled every ship not on Ship keys and threw away the spin `NavDockRcs` set. A ship in an order's close work (`ThrustPuppet`) keeps its own attitude in `SpacePose`; a close-work turn is checked by logging its angle off the goal over the approach, not by the docking ending.
- 2026-11-18: a Board order's Limpet let go under the Zero Day in orbit hung there (PR 318 had made contacts reach by closing speed, with a posed ship's own velocity): a ship out of the bubble is posed after the step, where it will be at the step's end, so a loose body still at the step's start met it a tick's travel on (3.5 m at 208 m/s) and the pod was jammed in the keel, held by friction. Rule: between a posed ship and a body not carried in its frame, meet the ship at its pose less its velocity times the step (`ConstructPhysics.PosedBack`); its riders (SpaceRide) are placed at its new pose and meet it as it is.
- 2026-11-18: a piece held in the physics gun aboard a moving ship sat a tick of the ship's travel ahead of the crosshair (0.33 m at 20 m/s, 4.7 m in the Rust Bucket's orbit), into the walls, and on a round world's side it turned the wrong way as the look swept (110 deg off after a half turn): `GrabTick` led the beam's point by the frame's velocity times dt on top of giving the piece that velocity, and the hold's turn was a yaw about the world's Y. The beam's point is where the look is now, the frame's motion is given once (`Follow`'s frameVel), and a look's yaw is a turn in the gravity frame at the eye (`ConstructGrab.YawQ(at, yaw)`, `LookQ`); try with `--ride-check NAME --held --speed V` (stepped and posed) and the window tool's `swing` on a planetoid's side (`--spawn-at 4300,0,4300` on Caledon), host and joined client.
- 2026-11-19: a joined client standing in the docked Limpet in orbit fell out at rest when its floor came off and died: a carried body is `Asleep`, so `SurfaceVel` gave 0 though `ConstructCargo.Follow` moves it at the ship's speed. Whether a body moves is `ConstructRide.Still` (anchored, or asleep and not carried), never `Asleep` alone.
