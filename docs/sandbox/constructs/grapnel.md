# The Grapnel salvage tug

The Salvagers' salvage tug, from Karl's picture (2026-11-21, "add this ship to the salvage ships"): a boxy hull in yard yellow and worn cream, a glass cockpit over a flat yellow chin carrying two claw arms and a cutting wheel, dark ring frames and a dark belt along the beam, four banded tanks with octagonal caps on each flank, a dish, whips, a radome, vents and skylights on the roof, four legs. Built as a draft for Karl's look (no recipes yet). It is the one Salvager ship not in their zinc-and-lime look: a hull a crew bought second hand and never repainted (Salvagers board, LOT 04).

- Generator: `Source/PartKit/Generators/GrapnelParts.cs` (`--gen-parts grapnel_parts`), started from the Satchel's CourierParts.cs (same plan, helpers and split into welded pieces); parts in `Constructs/Parts/Grapnel`, blueprint `Constructs/Blueprints/GrapnelSalvageTug.json` ("Grapnel Salvage Tug"). Pieces are blueprint-only (Categories.json's Ship name rule).
- Size: 35.5 m over the claws and drives, 11.6 m over the tank racks, about 125 t. Hull stations: a cut-cornered box (`Body`, half width 4.3, roof edge at 4.0 m), lower and narrower at the chin (`Zt`), whose flat face runs up to the beam with the windscreen over it (`Bow`).
- Drives: two Drive Nozzle M (5 MN each) on shrouds through the transom (`Grapnel Stern`, a `Works.Mount`), each with a Drive Core M and a Fuel Pump M on the stern bulkhead's inner face, standing in engineering at x +-2 (the Dray's pattern). Four lift jets (1.2 MN) and four S leg pods under the keel; Heavy RCS at the bow (roof, keel, cab flanks, a second pitch pair on the chin block) and the stern.
- Flight: the claws make a heavy nose, so the flight computer's config sets `strength` 150 (at 100 the nose came round 38 degrees of the check's 55); gyro 40 MN m.
- Cab and bay are the Satchel's (cockpit, bunk room, head, galley, airlock over the belly hatch; two Standard Container 2m on twist locks in a bay open to the sky). The bay's walls run up to the roof (`BayTop` 3.9). The power board, MMU dock, suit and Pressure Locker hang on the bay's aft bulkhead, facing engineering.
- Tank racks (`Grapnel Tank Rack L/R`): hang on hardpoints on the bay's flanks (`rackL`/`rackR`), fuel tanks of 3000 l each with a Fuel out.
- The claws and the cutter are look only: they don't move, grab or cut.
- Checks run (2026-11-21): `--check-vehicle` (0 coplanar faces, 0 slivers, both works at 100%; the wheel and drive lines are ground-vehicle checks), `--flight-check` (flies, nose 59 down, lift 2.79x), `--ladder-check` (up and down), `--selftest-only "No z-fighting"` and `"Compound collision"` (pass), `--check-construct-parts` (no problems). `--check-building` finds 0 m2 reachable, as it does on the Satchel on main (the belly-hatch boarding isn't followed by the check).
- Not done: Assembling recipes (after Karl's look), working claws and cutter, a place in the Salvagers' crews.
