# The Folio shuttle

The Traders' shuttle (Karl, 2026-11-21: "Build me a new ship using this system ... the traders' faction, a shuttle"), and the first ship on the ship kit (ship-kit.md). It carries a firm's people and its clients between a station and a ship, or down to a landing field.

- Generator: `Source/PartKit/Generators/FolioParts.cs` (`--gen-parts folio_parts`); parts in `Constructs/Parts/Folio` (38, blueprint-only by Categories.json's Ship name rule), blueprint `Constructs/Blueprints/FolioShuttle.json` ("Folio Shuttle").
- Size about 5.5 m across and 16.5 m long over the drives, 32 t. Two Folio Drives (300 kN each) on the transom and four Folio Lift Jets (220 kN each) under the keel: all under 500 kN, so no works. Fuel: 3200 in the keel, 1600 in the stern for the drives. Four leg pods (small), RCS fore, aft, up and down.
- Look (the Traders' office style, as the Satchel and the Docket wear it): an old office-case cream skin over a greyer lower hull, a corporate blue glasshouse band with a window by each row of couches, a glass nose cap, a white registry plate with the blue square by the door, square louvred lift jets and the drives in office-case housings with yellow corner tabs.
- Inside, nose to tail: a glass cockpit (two Flight Couches, the pilot wired to the Folio Flight Computer); a client cabin with six couches in three rows each side of a blue runner, a coffee station and a locker on the aft wall; the utility room through a door to port of the aisle, with the belly hatch over its shaft and the boarding ladder, the Engineer's Panel, the Power Board, the Folio Power Plant (160 kW, two cells in a narrow cage against the port wall), two batteries, a suit locker, Life Support and an Oxygen Tank.
- Checks run: `--check-vehicle` (0 coplanar faces among 1818 boxes), `--flight-check` (flies; climbed 11.5 m in 2 s, hover drift 0.4 m, lift 2.08x), `--ladder-check` (up and down ok), `--check-building` (ok, 11 m2), `--selftest-only "No z-fighting, Compound collision, Assembling"`.
- Not built: the Folio in the Traders' routes, a pressure door between the cabin and the utility room (the hatch is the airlock's only seal).
