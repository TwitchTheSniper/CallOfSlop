# The Docket tug

The Traders' tug (Karl, 2026-11-21: "a tug for the trader faction ... a new design that feels very trader", taking from no ship but the Traders' own, the Packet and the Satchel). The Traders' mood board's "tug and string": a stubby head that does the work and an open steel string it tows, each cell of the string taking one standard 2 m container ("Standard Container 2m", 2.5 x 2.5 x 6.5 m) on twist locks on its top, so a dock's crane lifts it straight off.

- Generator: `Source/PartKit/Generators/TugParts.cs` (`--gen-parts tug_parts`), built as the Satchel and the Packet are (their helpers copied); parts in `Constructs/Parts/Docket`, blueprint `Constructs/Blueprints/DocketTug.json` ("Docket Tug"). The pieces are blueprint-only (Categories.json's Ship name rule).
- Size: the head is 8 m across the hull (15 m over the nacelles) and 17 m long; with the yoke, three 6.9 m cells and the end car the whole ship is about 41 m, 157 t. Four Drive Nozzle M in four nacelles stacked two a side on outriggers (a tug is all engine), each with Drive Core M, Fuel Pump M and a hatch in its outboard flank for an EVA; four lift jets (1.7 MN each) and four leg pods under the head, two leg pods under the end car; Heavy RCS on the bow, the nacelles and the end car.
- Look (the Traders' IT style, look.md): beige office-case plating, case vents and drive-bay panels on the push bow, corporate blue bands, lanyard yellow bollards and rails, black rubber fenders on the bow; the string is the Packet's rack steel.
- Head: a rubber-fendered push bow; the cab with the bunk room and the head to port, the galley and the airlock (two suit lockers, Life Support, an O2 tank, the belly hatch with its boarding ladder) to starboard; engineering aft (power plant, three batteries, Engineer's Panel, tools, a suit, a Pressure Locker, an MMU dock); a raised glass wheelhouse on engineering's ceiling, reached by a ladder, glass all round so the pilot sees the tow, two flight couches and the flight computer.
- String: behind the transom a tow yoke grips an open truss of three cells (`Docket String Cell 1..3`), each with four twist locks (`lock0..3`, medium sockets; a container hangs on `lock0` by `foot.0`), X-bracing, a railed catwalk down each side and an amber bay lamp. The blueprint loads cells 1 (green) and 2 (yellow) and leaves cell 3 empty, so the frame shows.
- Flight computer gyro: 20 MN m, as the Satchel's. A gyro is yaw only, and `ConstructFlight.Solve` turns off any row whose strongest effector is under 5% of the strongest torque; a 200 MN m gyro switched roll off (E and Q did nothing).
- Checks run: `--check-vehicle` (0 coplanar faces; its wheel and drive lines are ground-vehicle checks), `--selftest-only "No z-fighting, Compound collision"`, `--ladder-check "Docket Tug"` (both ladders up and down), `--check-construct-parts` (no REFUSED). `--flight-check`: lifts 2.58x its weight, hovers level, flies forward, rolls on E/Q; on the look-down test the nose stops at 42 degrees (55 asked) as the long bow meets the ground from the check's low hover (the Packet stops at 53 the same way). `--check-building` finds no way aboard by the belly hatch, as for the Satchel (the check's limit).
- Not built: recipes (after Karl's "add to build"), the tug in the Traders' routes, a string that couples and uncouples cells.
