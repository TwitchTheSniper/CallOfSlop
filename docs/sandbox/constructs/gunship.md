# The Picket gunship

The Militia's twin-hull gunship (Karl, 2026-11-21: a picture of a twin-hull ship in tan and grey with orange stripes, "find a home for this ship ... give it to one of the factions"). Given to the Militia: they hold Glacier Watch and had no ships in space, and the ship's turrets and small hold fit a patrol and escort ship that carries its own post's stores. A medium-small ship, the Zero Day as the reference.

- Generator: `Source/PartKit/Generators/GunshipParts.cs` (`--gen-parts gunship_parts`), started from the Satchel's CourierParts.cs; parts in `Constructs/Parts/Picket`, blueprint `Constructs/Blueprints/PicketGunship.json` ("Picket Gunship"). The pieces are blueprint-only (Categories.json's Ship name rule).
- Size about 33 m long over the centre hull (33.5 m over the sponsons) and 27 m across the nacelles, 271 t (the hull sections are 4 cm steel). Two Drive Nozzle M (5 MN each) with Drive Core M and Fuel Pump M in short nacelles on stub wings, four 1.8 MN lift jets under the keel, four legs under the sponsons (a wide stance), Heavy RCS on the bow, the stern, the nacelles and both ends of each sponson.
- The centre hull is the Satchel's layout: a glass cockpit, a crew cab (bunks, head, galley, airlock over the boarding shaft), a bay open to the sky with two Standard Container 2m on twist locks (olive and sand), engineering aft.
- The sponsons (Karl, 2026-11-21, second pass: "more in line with the one I sent ... using the production system"): two big boxy hulls as tall as the centre hull (5.2 m across, 4.8 m high), skinned in **Hull Sections**, the production hull pieces made at run time from their keys (`HullLoft.Section.cs`, docs/sandbox/design/hull-dress.md), dressed in the `militia` style added to `Constructs/DressStyles.json` (tan and grey plates over a dark skin, dark chine rails, an orange strip over the beam). Each sponson is five sections a side (`SpSections`: a tapered nose, then four between black bands), a port and a starboard half each, hung by their facet 4 inside socket (`i4`) on a core piece (`Picket Sponson L/R Fore/Aft`, cut at `SpCut`). The cores are hidden boxes kept clear of the sections' voids (`SectionVoid`), the bands (the gaps between sections filled and drawn dark, a black band lapping both ends), the big square intake in the blunt bow, the stern plate, a leg mount, pitch jets, the webs to the cab (`sponLF`/`sponRF`) and engineering (`sponL`/`sponR`) through the bands, the nacelle's stub wing (`nac`) and the aft piece's turret pad (`gun`). A Panel Former doesn't make Hull Sections yet (hull-dress.md step 4), so `--bill` doesn't list them.
- Guns: three Zero Day Autocannons, each on a Turret Ring S (its trunk down a well in the pad) and a Motor Hinge, a box of rounds on the gun. The copilot works the one on the cab's roof (a Turret Module on the cockpit's back wall); a gunner at the Gunnery Console in engineering works the two on the sponsons (the module sits on the centreline so both wires stay inside 8 m).
- Flight: the gyro is 45e6 and the flight computer's pitch response 200: the sponsons' steel and length made the nose too slow on Ship keys at the defaults.
- The Militia's run: Crew/Stations.json has a Militia hauler route, Glacier Watch to Caledon Highport (steel out, iron ore and hydrogen back), one Picket hauling and a second riding escort.
- Checks run: `--check-vehicle` (0 coplanar faces between parts; its wheel and drive lines are ground-vehicle checks; the works lines note the rings have no magazine feed, the guns feed from their boxes), `--flight-check` (flies, lift 1.68x, the nose 59 degrees down on the look), `--ladder-check "Picket Gunship"`, `--selftest-only "No z-fighting,Compound collision,Assembling,Constructs"`. Each section key also passes `--hull-section KEY` (no coplanar faces).
- Not built: Assembling recipes for its pieces (the Satchel got them when Karl asked to add it to the build), magazines feeding the rings, the Picket in the Militia controller's fleet (its fleets are ground vehicles for now).
