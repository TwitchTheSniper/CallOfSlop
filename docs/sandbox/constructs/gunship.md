# The Picket gunship

The Militia's twin-hull gunship (Karl, 2026-11-21: a picture of a twin-hull ship in tan and grey with orange stripes, "find a home for this ship ... give it to one of the factions"). Given to the Militia: they hold Glacier Watch and had no ships in space, and the ship's turrets and small hold fit a patrol and escort ship that carries its own post's stores. A medium-small ship, the Zero Day as the reference.

- Generator: `Source/PartKit/Generators/GunshipParts.cs` (`--gen-parts gunship_parts`), started from the Satchel's CourierParts.cs; parts in `Constructs/Parts/Picket`, blueprint `Constructs/Blueprints/PicketGunship.json` ("Picket Gunship"). The pieces are blueprint-only (Categories.json's Ship name rule).
- Size about 33 m long over the centre hull (37 m over the sponsons) and 24 m across the nacelles, 150 t. Two Drive Nozzle M (5 MN each) with Drive Core M and Fuel Pump M in the nacelles, four 1.35 MN lift jets under the keel, four legs under the sponsons (a wide stance), Heavy RCS on the bow, the stern, the nacelles and both ends of each sponson.
- The centre hull is the Satchel's layout: a glass cockpit, a crew cab (bunks, head, galley, airlock over the boarding shaft), a bay open to the sky with two Standard Container 2m on twist locks (olive and sand), engineering aft.
- The sponsons: a loft of their own each (`Spon`, drawn with the centre hull's facet helpers through `WithLoft`), cut into a fore and an aft piece at `SpCut`. The aft piece hangs on engineering's web (`sponL`/`sponR`), the fore piece on the aft piece's bow end, and the build welds the fore piece to the cab's web (`sponLF`/`sponRF`). Each has an intake in its blunt bow, dark bands, the orange stripe down its outer flank, a leg mount and pitch jets at its far end; the aft piece carries the nacelle's pylon (`nac`) and a turret pad (`gun`).
- Guns: three Zero Day Autocannons, each on a Turret Ring S (its trunk down a well in the pad) and a Motor Hinge, a box of rounds on the gun. The copilot works the one on the cab's roof (a Turret Module on the cockpit's back wall); a gunner at the Gunnery Console in engineering works the two on the sponsons (the module sits on the centreline so both wires stay inside 8 m).
- Flight: the gyro is 26e6 and the flight computer's pitch response 150: the sponsons' length made the nose too slow on Ship keys (54 of 55 degrees) at the defaults.
- The Militia's run: Crew/Stations.json has a Militia hauler route, Glacier Watch to Caledon Highport (steel out, iron ore and hydrogen back), one Picket hauling and a second riding escort.
- Checks run: `--check-vehicle` (0 coplanar faces between parts; its wheel and drive lines are ground-vehicle checks; the works lines note the rings have no magazine feed, the guns feed from their boxes), `--flight-check` (flies, lift 2.44x, the nose 58 degrees down on the look), `--ladder-check "Picket Gunship"`, `--selftest-only "No z-fighting,Compound collision,Assembling,Constructs"`.
- Not built: Assembling recipes for its pieces (the Satchel got them when Karl asked to add it to the build), magazines feeding the rings, the Picket in the Militia controller's fleet (its fleets are ground vehicles for now).
