**Faults:**
- **A station can't take a store part into its hold** (item 2): the hold is a rack, and `ConstructConveyor.cs` ~247 refuses any `PartRole.Storage` part ("a rack takes pieces, not stores", line 263). In survival an Ammo Container, and likely any bin, can then reach a station build only by the station's own order at an Assembler. Whether the made one can reach the tray by that path wasn't traced: the showcase's Assembler had no rubber. `station give` with one in the hands didn't take it either. **This looks like a fault** (a blueprint with an Ammo Container may never finish at a station). The test's layout is the less likely cause. It is in the logistics and station owners' code, so it was not changed here.

**Seen in passing (the window tool, not the game):**
- The tool's `station stock` ignores pieces already riding to the tray and the piece in a Loader Arm's hand, so it leaves a spare part (here a Square Beam 2 m). It does nothing while the job is still Costing, so give it a frame after `station start`.
- `lifter joined` never saw the joined player as in for 15 s (it waited out the host's 400 s cap), although the client had joined and worked. `wait N` was used instead. Its cap `_wsLiftWaits < 6000` at 30 frames a wait is about 50 minutes, not the 3 its comment says.
- `PART#n` in steps is the n-th part of that name, but `station tanks` and `link` print a part's index (Fuel Tank#226 is `Fuel Tank#2`).

**Tool added:** `station cutpipe` and `station mendpipe` (`Program.WindowShot.cs`, inside the `station` case, host): every pipe into the station is cut through, as a blast leaves it, or repaired whole. The step-name line is unchanged.
