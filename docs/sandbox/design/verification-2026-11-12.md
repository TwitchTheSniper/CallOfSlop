# Verification run, 2026-11-12 (main at 334d5fad, protocol 271)

These are the behaviours that today's six merges left untried at run time. Each was tried in a throwaway run on the Windows PC with `--save-dir` set to a temp folder. The test blueprints were scratch files copied into `bin/` and removed afterwards; none are committed. For the "real click" items the input was genuine Win32 `SetCursorPos` + `mouse_event` / `keybd_event` sent to the game window. Computer use can't target the game, because it runs under `dotnet` and isn't an installed app.

| 1 | A pressed Drive part driving a vehicle | **Works** | The 4x4 Truck with its Automatic Gearbox swapped for `Pressed Part (drive.auto solid 50x50x50 steel)`: `--check-vehicle` gives 4 driven wheels and drives 41.7 m at 11.5 m/s in 6 s. The catalogue gearbox gives 77.8 m at 17.9 m/s; why the pressed one is slower was not looked into. Pressed leaf springs (`spring.leaf solid 15x55x90 steel`) x4 drive 78.2 m at 18.0 m/s.
